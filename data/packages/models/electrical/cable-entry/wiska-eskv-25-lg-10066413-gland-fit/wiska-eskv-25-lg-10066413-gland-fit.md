# WISKA ESKV 25 LG gland installation reference

Revision 1. Installation reference in millimetres. The gland shoulder is Z = 0. The thread region is Z = −10 to 0 mm. The external body projection is Z = 0 to +37 mm.

1. **Manufacturer dimensions.** WISKA order 10066413, M25 × 1.5 mm, 10 mm screw-in length, 38–47 mm total length, wrench size 29 mm and collar diameter 32 mm. The manufacturer gives a Ø9–17 mm cable range. [WISKA ESKV 25 data sheet](https://www.wiska.com/en/pdf/10066413/ESKV-25.pdf)
2. **Geometry represented.** The thread region is a smooth Ø25 mm solid. The cap-side region projects the union of a regular 29 mm wrench hexagon and Ø32 mm collar circle to the 47 mm total-length endpoint. It is a screening shape for nominal plate stack and packaging. The region has no internal cable passage and models neither the thread form nor the detailed cap profile.
3. **Checks.** One closed native solid. The mounting cylinder spans 10 mm. The reference projects 37 mm beyond the shoulder, with maximum radial extent 16.7432 mm. DWG SHA-256 `2414598cff09f5e69829f6bfed96ccf8709ac2f2ffc8f5e8ed0a86102bab4a59`.
