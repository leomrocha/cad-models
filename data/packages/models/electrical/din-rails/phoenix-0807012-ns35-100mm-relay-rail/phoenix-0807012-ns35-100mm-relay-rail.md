## Geometry

Proposed 100 mm cut of Phoenix Contact 0807012 perforated NS 35/7.5 rail. The retained section has a 1 mm backing and four 15 × 6.2 mm slots at 25 mm pitch.

Millimetres. U along the rail, V down and N toward the door. Bearing face N = 0.

The native section is 35 × 7.5 mm. The lip bends have R0.8 and R1.8 mm radii. The cut stations in the retained source STEP are −23.8 and 76.2 mm along its original length axis. The four slot centres in the cut part are U = −37.5, −12.5, 12.5 and 37.5 mm.
