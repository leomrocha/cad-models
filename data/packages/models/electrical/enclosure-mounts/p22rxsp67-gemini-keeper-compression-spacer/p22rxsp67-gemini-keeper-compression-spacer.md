# P22RXSP67 Tyro Gemini keeper compression spacer

Revision 1. Discussion construction proposal. Dimensions are in millimetres. The tube axis is local +Z and the carrier-facing end plane is Z = 0.

1. **Tube.** Steel, nominal Ø12 outside, Ø6.6 bore, cut length 67 mm. The design proposes a 66.9 to 67.1 mm receiving band and square parallel ends.
2. **Use.** Four copies set the face-to-face spacing between the receiver carrier and the outer keeper. The steel spacers carry the assembly clamp load around the receiver enclosure. The 5 mm back pad, 60 mm case depth and 2 mm front pad form the nominal installation stack.
3. **Checks.** One closed solid with two coaxial cylindrical surfaces. The four boundary rings match the two diameters and both end stations. DWG SHA-256 `e9687703006433cc47167b796e9b9e8790a3f2f3f362c6b9f7d77ebe06753f7b`.
