## Geometry

R2 cabinet crossmember receiver block with two nominal M6 thread envelopes on 20 mm pitch and bevels at the rear-wall corners.

Millimetres. X along screw penetration, Y along beam, Z up from beam centre.

The receiver is 12 mm deep, 24 mm wide and 31 mm high. The upper nominal thread envelope meets the bevel at the entrance. Confirm the lead-in, usable engagement and receiver retention on the manufactured geometry.
