# P22RXB Tyro Gemini 1S forward receiver bridge

Revision 1. Discussion construction proposal. Dimensions are in millimetres. X points forward, Y points left and Z points up.

1. **Finished shape.** 3 mm steel formed bridge, 60 mm along X from -205 to -145. Native bend centres are (Y, Z) = (176, 537) and (196, 546). Both bends have R3 inside and R6 outside. The inner bearing face is Y = 170 and the outer bearing face is Y = 202.
2. **Hole pattern.** Ø6.6 mm normal to Y at (X, Z) = (-175, 520) for the rail and (-155, 563) for the carrier. The two axes are 20 mm apart in X. The rim to straight X edge at the carrier hole is 6.7 mm.
