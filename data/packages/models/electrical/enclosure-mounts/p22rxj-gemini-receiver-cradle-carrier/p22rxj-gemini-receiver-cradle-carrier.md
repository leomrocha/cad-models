# P22RXJ Tyro Gemini 1S external cradle carrier

Revision 1. Discussion construction proposal. Dimensions are in millimetres. X points forward, Y points left and Z points up.

1. **Plate.** 200 × 174 × 3 mm proposed steel carrier. X = −345 to −145, Y = 202 to 205 and Z = 476 to 650. The source Tyro Gemini 1S case is 160 × 150 × 60 mm. [Tyro technical specification](https://www.tyroremotes.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Gemini-EN-1.1.pdf)
2. **Bridge and seat holes.** At each of X = −335 and −155, the Ø6.6 mm bridge hole is at Z = 563 and the Ø4.5 mm bottom-seat hole is at Z = 490. All holes pass through the carrier normal to Y.
3. **Keeper slots.** At each of X = −335 and −155, the lower Ø6.6 mm slot has semicircle centres at Z = 510 and 525, and the upper slot has centres at Z = 590 and 620. The nearest bridge-hole to slot gaps are 31.4 mm and 20.4 mm. The minimum straight X-edge ligament is 6.7 mm.
4. **Checks.** One closed solid, 12 cylinder-axis and radius comparisons and 32 slot endpoint witnesses on the two plate faces. DWG SHA-256 `cab585d093adb5e43997f41db8e4cdd8e6a0f479d1cde286f89f0ea01a9443cd`.
