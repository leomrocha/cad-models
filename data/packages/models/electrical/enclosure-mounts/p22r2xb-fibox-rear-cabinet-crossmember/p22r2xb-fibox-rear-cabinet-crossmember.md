# R2 Fibox cabinet crossmember with four rear-wall passages

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

R2 cabinet support crossmember, 520 mm long, with a nominal 40 × 40 × 3 mm section and four Ø6.6 mm passages through its rear wall.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Cut length is along Y.

The tube ends sit at Y = ±260 mm. The bore axes are Y = ±150 mm at Z = 510 and 530 mm. The upper bore exits follow the inner corner curve and reach 0.015 mm past the flat wall station. The body retains 3 and 6 mm nominal inside and outside corner radii.
