# Fibox MPS 3020 plate with direct relay rail drilling

**Catalogue draft ID** `fibox-mps3020-direct-relay-rail-plate`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Machining proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](fibox-mps3020-direct-relay-rail-plate.dwg), SHA-256 `61bf406394fc5efe964cdb4f7f8fc1571230b963149c8e5f01d5569ca8eb93e7`.

## Geometry

Fibox MPS 3020 mounting plate with two proposed Ø5.5 mm bores on 75 mm pitch for a direct-mounted relay rail. The plate retains its four source Ø6 mm bores.

Millimetres. U along the rail, V down, N toward the cabinet door. Rail bearing face N = 0.

The source plate is 150 × 250 × 2 mm, with four 32 mm corner chamfers and a 110 × 210 mm bore pattern. The four round holes follow the manufacturer STEP. The enclosure dimension drawing calls out 5.2 × 10 mm slots on the same centres.

**Source model revision** `fibox-arca-203015t-8121020` revision 1, SHA-256 `2938b38109e7481e9e73fa2c56924b5371e30ec7f7aeb9ab27ba93d8b10e6d82`.
