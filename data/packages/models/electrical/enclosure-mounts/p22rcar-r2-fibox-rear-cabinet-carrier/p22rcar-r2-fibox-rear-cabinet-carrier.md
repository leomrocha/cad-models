## Geometry

R2 mounting carrier for the rearward Fibox cabinet layout. The 320 × 320 × 3 mm plate has four source-foot axes and four proposed support axes.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Rear foot contact plane X = −460 mm.

The eight Ø6.6 mm bores are parallel to X. The proposed support-bore edge land is 6.7 mm. This plate uses a rearward carrier support and the existing cabinet foot pattern.
[Fibox source cabinet](../../enclosures/fibox-arca-203015t-8121020/fibox-arca-203015t-8121020.md).
