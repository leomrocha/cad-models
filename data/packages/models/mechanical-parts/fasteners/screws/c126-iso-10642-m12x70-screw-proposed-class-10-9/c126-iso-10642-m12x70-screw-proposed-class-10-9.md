## Geometry

Nominal M12 countersunk screw envelope. Overall length is 70 mm, physical head diameter is 24.81 mm, and the reference cone is 90 degrees.

Millimetres. The physical head top is Z = 0. The nominal shaft points along +Z.
Nominal external and mounting-envelope geometry. Thread shape, fillets and insert details use source hardware inspection.

The separate theoretical cone diameter is 26.88 mm. The nominal hex socket is 8 mm across flats and 4.3 mm deep in this reference. Installed head seating uses the actual head and neck geometry.
