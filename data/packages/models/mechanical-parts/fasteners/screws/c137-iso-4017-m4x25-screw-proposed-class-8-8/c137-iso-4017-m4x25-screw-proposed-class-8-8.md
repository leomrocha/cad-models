# ISO 4017 M4 × 25 screw nominal reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `c137-iso-4017-m4x25-screw-proposed-class-8-8`. Revision 1.

[Native DWG 2018](c137-iso-4017-m4x25-screw-proposed-class-8-8.dwg), SHA-256 `4581f3d2e9d1e9d60fa2a7fcc9469634e55c6176dde071f000ddcdf8bd5af1e0`.

## Geometry

Nominal M4 × 25 screw with a 7 mm across-flats head and a Ø4 mm shaft envelope.

Axis +Z from the bearing face.

Head height is 2.8 mm. The bearing face is at Z = 0 mm and tip at Z = 25 mm. The solid is a fit-check envelope with cylindrical nominal thread geometry.

[ISO 4017:2022 sample](https://cdn.standards.iteh.ai/samples/72585/1179998ddd594550a909d6e13c416f3e/ISO-4017-2022.pdf).
