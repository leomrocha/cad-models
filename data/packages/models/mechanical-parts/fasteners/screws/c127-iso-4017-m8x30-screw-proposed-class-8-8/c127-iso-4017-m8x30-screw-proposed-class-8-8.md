# ISO 4017 M8 × 30 screw nominal reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `c127-iso-4017-m8x30-screw-proposed-class-8-8`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Nominal discussion reference.

[Native DWG 2018](c127-iso-4017-m8x30-screw-proposed-class-8-8.dwg), SHA-256 `08591e1eda4ff310a886908e4cae407d287378af51d30190c087cf30a803bb2d`.

## Geometry

Nominal M8 screw envelope with a 30 mm under-head length, a 13 mm across-flats head and a 5.3 mm head height.

Millimetres. The underside of the head is Z = 0. The shaft points along +Z.
Nominal external and mounting-envelope geometry. Thread shape, fillets and insert details use source hardware inspection.
