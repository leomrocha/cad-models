# J3f Kelly outboard M6 × 45 screw reference

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

Proposed nominal M6 × 45 hex-head screw geometry for the J3f outboard carrier joint.

Millimetres. +Z along the screw from the under-head datum to the tip.

Head and shaft diameter follow the nominal M6 source model. The new 45 mm under-head length is a joint proposal. The shaft is a simple thread envelope.
