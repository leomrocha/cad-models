# ISO 4017 M12 × 40 screw nominal reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `c162-iso-4017-m12x40-screw-proposed-class-8-8`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Nominal discussion reference.

[Native DWG 2018](c162-iso-4017-m12x40-screw-proposed-class-8-8.dwg), SHA-256 `6ceb98b4ad7a8a9de02ad451226aa814cc72f9de81b39261f0c16943ae949929`.

## Geometry

Nominal fully threaded M12 screw envelope with 40 mm under-head length, 18 mm head across flats and 7.5 mm head height.

Millimetres. The underside of the head is Z = 0. The shaft points along +Z.
Nominal external and mounting-envelope geometry. Thread shape, fillets and insert details use source hardware inspection.
