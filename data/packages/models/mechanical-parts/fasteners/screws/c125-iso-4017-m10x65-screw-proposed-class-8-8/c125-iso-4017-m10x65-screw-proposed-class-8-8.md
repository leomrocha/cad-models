# ISO 4017 M10 × 65 screw nominal reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `c125-iso-4017-m10x65-screw-proposed-class-8-8`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Nominal discussion reference.

[Native DWG 2018](c125-iso-4017-m10x65-screw-proposed-class-8-8.dwg), SHA-256 `0f4078a5a43da86382cff23cc97e871ff7b7d99398bef63745a158b850cfa07a`.

## Geometry

Nominal M10 screw envelope with a 65 mm under-head length, a 16 mm across-flats head and a 6.4 mm head height.

Millimetres. The underside of the head is Z = 0. The shaft points along +Z.
Nominal external and mounting-envelope geometry. Thread shape, fillets and insert details use source hardware inspection.
