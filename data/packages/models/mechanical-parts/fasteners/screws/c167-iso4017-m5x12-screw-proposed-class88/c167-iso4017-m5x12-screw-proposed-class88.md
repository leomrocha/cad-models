# ISO 4017 M5 × 12 screw nominal reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `c167-iso4017-m5x12-screw-proposed-class88`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Nominal discussion reference.

[Native DWG 2018](c167-iso4017-m5x12-screw-proposed-class88.dwg), SHA-256 `90ff7a63e2e589a295025fb89b8c8d6cc2f2bcf46ecfb1f95ea28ba2d0e9f9eb`.

## Geometry

Nominal M5 screw envelope with 12 mm under-head length, 8 mm head across flats and 3.5 mm head height.

Millimetres. The underside of the head is Z = 0. The shaft points along +Z.
Nominal external and mounting-envelope geometry. Thread shape, fillets and insert details use source hardware inspection.
