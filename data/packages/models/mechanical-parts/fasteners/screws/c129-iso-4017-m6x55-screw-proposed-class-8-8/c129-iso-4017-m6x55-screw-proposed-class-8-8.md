# ISO 4017 M6 × 55 screw nominal reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `c129-iso-4017-m6x55-screw-proposed-class-8-8`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Nominal discussion reference.

[Native DWG 2018](c129-iso-4017-m6x55-screw-proposed-class-8-8.dwg), SHA-256 `016e29607e1b47d4b421a292483d4435c9d83aea8c51581507d39679c57bb386`.

## Geometry

Nominal M6 screw envelope with a 55 mm under-head length, 10 mm across-flats head and 4 mm head height.

Millimetres. The underside of the head is Z = 0. The shaft points along +Z.
Nominal external and mounting-envelope geometry. Thread shape, fillets and insert details use source hardware inspection.
