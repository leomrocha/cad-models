## Geometry

Nominal ISO 10642 M8 × 20 countersunk socket screw for the J4 inner shaft retainer.

Millimetres. Z = 0 at the flat head top and +Z points toward the screw tip.

The selected head is Ø16.43 mm with a 90° included angle. The modeled M8 shank is a diameter envelope. The hex socket is 5.08 mm across flats and 3 mm deep. The ideal sharp-cone recess in the nominal Ø18.25 mm washer is 0.91 mm.
