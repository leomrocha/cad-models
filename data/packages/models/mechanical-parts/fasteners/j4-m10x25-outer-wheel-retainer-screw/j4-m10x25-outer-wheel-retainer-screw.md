## Geometry

Nominal M10 × 25 hex-head screw proposal for the J4 outboard retaining stack.

Millimetres. Z = 0 on the head bearing face. +Z points toward the thread tip.

The nominal head is 16 mm across flats and 6.4 mm high. With a 5 mm washer, the proposed screw reaches 20 mm into the nominal 25 mm shaft-end cavity. The thread is represented by its diameter envelope.
