# J4 rear attachment crossmember doubler, 246 mm

**Catalogue draft ID** `p09d-j4-rear-attachment-doubler-246mm`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p09d-j4-rear-attachment-doubler-246mm.dwg), SHA-256 `14d4c72d49b4f17faef4e7980ddcce4b4956c91ed3738277fb222a4eb19c4c19`.

## Geometry

246 × 80 × 8 mm rear crossmember doubler with four Ø13 mm X-axis through-bores.

Millimetres. Common J4 frame with X forward, Y left, Z up.

The four bore axes sit at Y = ±100 mm and Z = 520 and 560 mm. The plate leaves a 1 mm nominal lateral gap to each inner caster-bridge root plate.
