**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

23 × 24 × 15 mm J4 left forward Kelly insert with a 13 mm blind M6 thread envelope.

Millimetres. X along the rail, Y up, +Z inward from the exit wall.

The closed block has a 13 mm nominal envelope and 2 mm of bottom stock. The entry face seats behind the outer rail wall. Retention and usable thread require their own construction inputs. The trimmed edge leaves a 5 mm nominal ligament to the bore.
