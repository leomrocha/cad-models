# J3f front transverse top-link cheek

**Catalogue draft ID** `j3f-front-transverse-top-link-cheek`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j3f-front-transverse-top-link-cheek.dwg), SHA-256 `9721a625d920c6a391dbfc45d4a4f9e37c28935c96e3d863d8834cbfbd074e4e`.

## Geometry

57 × 50 × 10 mm front top-link cheek with a Ø19.5 mm through bore. Two occurrences make the proposed transverse mount.

Millimetres. X runs outward from the carrier root, Y is up in the plate profile, Z is stock thickness.

The bore centre is 37 mm from the root and 25 mm from the lower edge.
The minimum nominal edge ligament is 10.25 mm.
The paired installation proposes a 52 mm gap between cheek faces. The gap is a packaging assumption for the selected linkage eye.
