# J3f Kelly outboard threaded rail insert

**Catalogue draft ID** `j3f-kelly-outboard-rail-insert`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j3f-kelly-outboard-rail-insert.dwg), SHA-256 `69d8cd42b1202be1481d0da482becf6bde4d135f499d5f27e9d5c98271869dc5`.

## Geometry

Proposed 30 × 24 × 15 mm J3f rail insert with a 13 mm blind M6 thread envelope.

Millimetres. X along the rail, Y up, +Z inward from the exit wall.

The closed block has a 13 mm nominal envelope and 2 mm of bottom stock. The entry face seats behind the outer rail wall. Retention and usable thread require their own construction inputs.
