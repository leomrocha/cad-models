## Geometry

J4 right motor backbone with the lower edge raised to Z = 308 mm.

Tractor installation frame in millimetres. X points forward, Y left and Z up.

The 23 mm lower-edge cut leaves the original 5 mm plate thickness. The four Ø11 mm bores, the pilot travel capsule and the four mounting screw travel capsules retain their original axes and checked boundary edges.
