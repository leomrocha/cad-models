## Geometry

Proposed 50 mm splash-panel crossbar. The open-ended steel tube has a 20 × 20 mm section, 2 mm walls and one Ø6.5 mm transverse fixing axis through both walls.

X along tube, Y along fixing axis, Z up. Origin at the minimum material corner.

Installed quantity per splash-panel pair is 4.
