# BMW B01 upper two-axis M8 threaded tab bridge

**Catalogue draft ID** `bmw-b01-upper-m8-threaded-tab-bridge`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](bmw-b01-upper-m8-threaded-tab-bridge.dwg), SHA-256 `a4c7bfcb7efce15a61723dec43cb9097775b540cece1732b0b72174a14892f9f`.

## Geometry

BMW mounting-tab bridge, 11 × 300 × 8 mm, with two M8 nominal thread envelopes at 278 mm pitch.

Millimetres. X across bridge width, Y between tab bores, Z up from bearing face.

The two screws share the bridge and prevent rotation once both are engaged. Each end bears on a battery mounting tab. The middle span clears the battery end plate.
The two cylindrical cuts represent nominal M8 thread envelopes. The joint requires a material, load and thread engagement check for manufacture.
