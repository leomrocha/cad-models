# J3f detachable recessed-captive deck ledge

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

600 × 25 × 3 mm recessed-captive deck ledge with two detachable cap seats at 536 mm pitch.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Mirror Y for the negative-Y ledge.

The two mounting axes are X = ±268 mm, Y = 187.5 mm. The 90° seats extend from Ø12.25 mm at Z = 767 mm to Ø18.25 mm at Z = 770 mm. The four captive cup passages and central seam seat remain in place.
