## Geometry

One 650 mm raised-frame steel rail, 40 × 40 mm nominal hollow section with 3 mm walls. Three Ø6.6 mm screw passages and six Ø3 mm plug-weld apertures pass through the upper wall.

Native X/Y are the section axes. Native Z follows the rail length.

The machining check preserves the eight source corner surfaces. All nine apertures exit at the two upper-wall faces.
