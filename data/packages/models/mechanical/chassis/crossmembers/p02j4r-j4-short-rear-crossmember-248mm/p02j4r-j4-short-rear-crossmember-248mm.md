## Geometry

J4 248 mm rear crossmember for the inboard caster root plates.

Millimetres. X and Y describe the tube section. Z measures length from the first end.

The 40 × 40 × 3 mm rounded square tube preserves the original 3 mm and 6 mm corner radii. The proposed installed end planes meet the inboard root plate faces at Y = ±124 mm.
