# J4 left raised chassis rail with offset guard mount bores

**Catalogue draft ID** `p01j650-j4-left-offset-guard-rail`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion machining proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p01j650-j4-left-offset-guard-rail.dwg), SHA-256 `ef81aa0d1f3056b25fd796656121d1eb3a438617dc0d79182c71541478ef7c45`.

## Geometry

650 mm raised J4 left rail with four offset guard mounting passages through both walls.

Stock coordinates in millimetres. Z along the 650 mm rail. X normal to the two cut walls.

The proposed 40 × 40 × 3 mm stock receives Ø6.5 mm bores at installed X = -88, -18, 62, 132 mm. Four axes give eight wall holes. The stock section, inner and outer opening trims and wall thickness were checked.

The nominal rail bore axes sit 22 mm forward of the guard-side fasteners. The native geometry contains only the four shifted axes.
