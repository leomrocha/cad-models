# J4 right raised rail with guard mount passages

**Catalogue draft ID** `p01j650-j4-right-guard-passage-rail`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion machining proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p01j650-j4-right-guard-passage-rail.dwg), SHA-256 `d68d79ad25b53d164851138d5cd290d52002ae6ad792589ace519a24f6259637`.

## Geometry

650 mm raised J4 right rail with four vertical guard mounting passages through both walls.

Stock coordinates in millimetres. Z along the 650 mm rail. X normal to the two cut walls.

The proposed 40 × 40 × 3 mm stock receives Ø6.5 mm bores at installed X = -110, 0, 110, 210 mm. Four axes give eight wall holes. The stock section, inner and outer opening trims and wall thickness were checked.
