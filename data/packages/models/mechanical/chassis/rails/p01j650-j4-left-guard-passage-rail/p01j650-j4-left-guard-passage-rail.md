## Geometry

650 mm raised J4 left rail with four vertical guard mounting passages through both walls.

Stock coordinates in millimetres. Z along the 650 mm rail. X normal to the two cut walls.

The proposed 40 × 40 × 3 mm stock receives Ø6.5 mm bores at installed X = -110, -40, 40, 110 mm. Four axes give eight wall holes. The stock section, inner and outer opening trims and wall thickness were checked.
