# J3f raised rail with foot and Kelly carrier passages

## Geometry

650 mm J3f rail with eight deck-foot passages and two Kelly carrier clamp passages through both sidewalls.

Stock coordinates in millimetres. Z along the 650 mm rail. Y normal to the two cut walls.

Both new Ø6.6 mm axes lie at Z = 520 mm after installation, at X = −137 and 77 mm. The eight Ø11 mm foot axes and the 40 × 40 × 3 mm rounded tube remain.
