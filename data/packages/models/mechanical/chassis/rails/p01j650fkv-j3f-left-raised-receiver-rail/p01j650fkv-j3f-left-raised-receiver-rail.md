# J3f left rail with vertical raised receiver passages

Revision 1. Discussion machining proposal for the raised Gemini receiver on the left J3f rail. Dimensions are in millimetres. Local Z follows the rail length. Local X is normal to the two newly drilled walls.

1. **Stock.** 650 mm long 40 × 40 × 3 mm rounded square hollow section. The deck-foot and Kelly carrier passages remain in the same positions.
2. **Receiver passages.** Two Ø6.6 mm bores at local (Y, Z) = (−8, 48) and (−8, 103). The installation transform puts them at chassis (X, Y) = (−277, 142) and (−222, 142), with the bores normal to Z. Wall faces are at Z = 500, 503, 537 and 540.
3. **Checks.** One closed native solid. Native axis and rim checks confirmed four new wall holes, 16 retained deck-foot wall holes and four retained Kelly wall holes. DWG SHA-256 `6eb1cfb4024f08ade49219393c9d117ddaed1df80c3bc3bc24383370fe4a9b4f`.
