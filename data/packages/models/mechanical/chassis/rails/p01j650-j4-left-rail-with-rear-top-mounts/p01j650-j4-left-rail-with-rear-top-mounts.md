# J4 left raised rail with rear top-mounted deck and lifting feet

**Catalogue draft ID** `p01j650-j4-left-rail-with-rear-top-mounts`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion machining proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p01j650-j4-left-rail-with-rear-top-mounts.dwg), SHA-256 `be5fc9596b0f98a6ebd3ffdc7c4731021f719eaae1777c1c65ab825375094cf3`.

## Geometry

650 mm J4 left rail with top-mounted rear feet and side-mounted front feet.

Stock coordinates in millimetres. Z along the 650 mm rail. X normal to the two cut walls.

The proposed 40 × 40 × 3 mm stock receives Ø6.5 mm bores at installed X = -88, -18, 62, 132 mm. Four axes give eight wall holes. The stock section, inner and outer opening trims and wall thickness were checked.

The nominal rail bore axes sit 22 mm forward of the guard-side fasteners.
Four rear Ø11 mm holes pass through the top wall only at X = −265, −235, −195 and −165 mm.
Four front Ø11 mm axes pass through both sidewalls at X = 165, 195, 235 and 265 mm. Native readback checked the position and both trim rings of every opening.
