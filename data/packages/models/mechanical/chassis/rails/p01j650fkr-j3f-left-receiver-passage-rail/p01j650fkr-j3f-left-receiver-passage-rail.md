# J3f left rail with foot, Kelly and receiver passages

Revision 1. Discussion machining proposal for the left J3f rail. Dimensions are in millimetres. Local Z follows the rail length, and local Y is normal to the two drilled walls.

1. **Stock.** 650 mm long 40 × 40 × 3 mm rounded square hollow section. The drilled rail retains its earlier deck-foot and Kelly carrier interfaces.
2. **Receiver passages.** Two Ø6.6 mm axes along local Y through both walls at local (X, Z) = (0, 10) and (0, 150). The left-rail placement maps them to chassis X = −315 and −175, Z = 520. The rear centre is 10 mm from the rail end, giving 6.7 mm of nominal material to that edge.
3. **Checks.** One closed native solid. Four new wall holes, 16 retained foot wall holes and four retained Kelly wall holes passed the native rim and axis checks. The hollow section and outer bounds were preserved. DWG SHA-256 `56704ef65664d3e301071ec8dfc01648f531bff4e1458acf7d000eef7bc39de6`.
