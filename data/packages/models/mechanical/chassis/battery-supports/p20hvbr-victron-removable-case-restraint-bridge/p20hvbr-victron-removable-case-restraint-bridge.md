# Victron removable upper case restraint bridge

**Catalogue draft ID** `p20hvbr-victron-removable-case-restraint-bridge`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p20hvbr-victron-removable-case-restraint-bridge.dwg), SHA-256 `06c12ead86c45635d26345e160f95b9530af63a56d0595d672ba0458c8c506c8`.

## Geometry

Proposed removable 420 mm bridge made from nominal 30 × 30 × 3 mm angle. Two Ø8.5 mm holes on 390 mm centres locate its ends.

Part-local millimetres. CAD X is normal to the mounting face, Y spans the angle flange, and Z follows the cut length.
The pads seat against the flat flange. At each bolt axis a nominal Ø16 mm washer envelope leaves 4 mm to the angle return and 7 mm to the cut end. Mounting posts and load capacity are separate checks.
