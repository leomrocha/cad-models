## Geometry

Leaf and Victron terminal hood split cable strip. Proposed insulating part, 40 × 20 × 3 mm, with 2 Ø4.5 mm through bores. A lower-edge R9 mm semicircle forms one half of the reference Ø18 mm opening.

Part-local millimetres. The origin is centered along the split edge. X runs along that edge and Y runs into the strip. Z spans the thickness.
The bore centers in local XY are (-15, 10), (15, 10) mm.

The matching wall makes the other half of the cable opening. The strip seats on the wall outer face. Place the opening using the installed cable centre line.
