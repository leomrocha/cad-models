# Victron outboard terminal hood angle mounting shoe

## Geometry

Proposed 17 mm cut of nominal 30 × 30 × 3 mm steel angle for the outboard Victron hood foot. Two Ø4.5 mm holes are spaced 10 mm apart in the flange.

Part-local millimetres. CAD X is normal to the mounting face, Y spans the angle flange, and Z follows the cut length.
The flange spans the full extent of the hood bracket foot. The nominal Ø9 mm washer envelopes leave 4 mm to the cut ends and 2.5 mm to the angle return. The return provides the connection face to a separate supporting frame.
