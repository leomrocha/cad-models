## Geometry

Victron terminal hood insulating support foot. Proposed insulating part, 20 × 20 × 3 mm, with 3 Ø4.5 mm through bores.

Part-local millimetres. X and Y are centered at the centre bore. X also joins the two shelf-extension bores. Z spans the thickness.
The bore centers in local XY are (-5, 0), (0, 0), (5, 0) mm.

This separate foot defines the mounting template for the integral-foot shelf extension.
