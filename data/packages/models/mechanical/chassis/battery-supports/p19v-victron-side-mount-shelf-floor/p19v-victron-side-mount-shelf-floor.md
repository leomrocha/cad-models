# Victron side-mount shelf floor reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `p19v-victron-side-mount-shelf-floor`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p19v-victron-side-mount-shelf-floor.dwg), SHA-256 `09d6f46a75c96e3f3d5eac37b5d0199867d8b9be83e1e5cd4dc44e395a22cd9c`.

## Geometry

Proposed 3 mm steel support floor for the side-mounted Victron battery case. The floor follows the drawing-based 341.2 × 234.7 mm case footprint.

Shelf-local millimetres. X follows the terminal pitch, Y follows the long case-height axis when rolled onto its side, and Z points upward. The top is at Z = 0 mm.
The surrounding ventilation slot is a separate 381.2 × 274.7 mm packaging boundary. Case contacts, shelf returns and the four M8 frame joint locations are separate design features.
