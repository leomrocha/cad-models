## Geometry

Victron terminal hood reference with 3 mm walls and roof, a 40 × 40 × 30 mm cavity and two Ø4.5 mm support bores spaced 66 mm apart.

Part-local millimetres. CAD X is u, Y is v and Z is n. The origin is on the terminal seating plane and n points outward.
The cover body is 46 × 46 × 33 mm. The support ears extend the overall width to 86 mm. The nominal lug envelope is an assumed 20 × 20 × 20 mm packaging input. The two ears seat at n = 30 mm. Cable openings use installed cable geometry.
