## Geometry

Leaf terminal cover outboard cassette extension bracket. The proposed 3 mm formed Z-section is 40 mm wide. Two bends use R3 inside and R6 outside radii. Four Ø4.5 mm bores join the floor and upright.

Local CAD axes X = v (height), Y = n (normal to the module face), Z = u offset from the strip centre.
The upper return seats on the cassette underside at v = −3 mm. The lower shelf supports the upright at v = −16 mm. The nominal upper washer margin to the bend tangent is 2.5 mm.
