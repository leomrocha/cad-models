## Geometry

30 × 30 × 3 mm restraint post cap with one centred Ø8.5 mm bore.

Part-local millimetres. X increases across the footprint, Y spans width, Z starts at the component bottom.
The nominal bridge seat is Z = 164.216 mm above the shelf datum. The open angle permits access to the underside of the cap. The base connection remains a separate frame interface.
