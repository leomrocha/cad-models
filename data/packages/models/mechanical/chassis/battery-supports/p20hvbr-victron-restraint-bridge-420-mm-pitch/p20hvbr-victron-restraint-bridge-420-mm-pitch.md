# Victron removable restraint bridge on 420 mm mounting centres

**Catalogue draft ID** `p20hvbr-victron-restraint-bridge-420-mm-pitch`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p20hvbr-victron-restraint-bridge-420-mm-pitch.dwg), SHA-256 `e2a580ea6490a146a1893a549dfaf88ce05c33c84aee115a7de43c015f962eeb`.

## Geometry

Proposed removable 450 mm bridge made from nominal 30 × 30 × 3 mm angle. Two Ø8.5 mm holes on 420 mm centres locate its ends.

Part-local millimetres. CAD X is normal to the mounting face, Y spans the angle flange, and Z follows the cut length.
The pads seat against the flat flange. At each bolt axis a nominal Ø16 mm washer envelope leaves 4 mm to the angle return and 7 mm to the cut end. Mounting posts and load capacity are separate checks.

A centred 30 mm post at each end leaves 24.397 mm to the projected case envelope.
