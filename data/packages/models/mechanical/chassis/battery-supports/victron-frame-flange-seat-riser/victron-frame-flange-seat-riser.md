# Victron frame flange seat riser

**Catalogue draft ID** `victron-frame-flange-seat-riser`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](victron-frame-flange-seat-riser.dwg), SHA-256 `91d43254c6a4dc21bde61a1148e858c1f82777b842b9822791ddb34bb38e3343`.

## Geometry

16 × 18 × 21 mm riser for a cantilevered battery shelf seat tab.

Part-local millimetres. X extends toward the supporting crossbar, Y spans the seat, and Z starts at the lower face.

Use four identical parts per supported shelf. The tab projects from the crossbar riser for fastener access.
