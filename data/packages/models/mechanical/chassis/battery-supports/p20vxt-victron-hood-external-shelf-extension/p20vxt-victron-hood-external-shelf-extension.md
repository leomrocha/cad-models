## Geometry

20 mm wide, 3 mm thick Victron terminal hood extension with two R3 inside bends. One Ø4.5 mm bore seats the insulating post and two Ø4.5 mm bores fasten the shelf foot.

Millimetres. CAD X is post outward n, Y is the outboard direction u from the post axis, and Z is the transverse strip coordinate v.
The assumed shelf plane is n = −40 mm. The post seats at n = 23 mm. The upper bore is at (u, v) = (33, 0) mm and the shelf bores are at (95.1, ±5) mm. The illustrative 60.6 mm case-edge offset sets the upright at u = 80.6 mm.
