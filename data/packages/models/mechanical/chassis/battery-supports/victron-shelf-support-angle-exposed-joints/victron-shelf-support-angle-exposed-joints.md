## Geometry

450 mm shelf support from nominal 30 × 30 × 3 mm angle stock, with two Ø8.5 mm through bores at 365.6 mm pitch.

Part-local millimetres. X measures depth from the top flange, Y spans the flange, and Z follows the cut length.

The bore centres are 42.2 mm and 407.8 mm from the cut end. Each nominal 16 mm washer has 4 mm clearance to the angle return.
