# Victron inboard terminal hood mounting angle

## Geometry

Proposed 17 mm cut of 30 × 30 × 3 mm steel angle for a Victron inboard terminal hood post. The single post bore is perpendicular to the pair of bores on the return.

Part-local millimetres. CAD X is normal to the mounting face, Y spans the angle flange, and Z follows the cut length.
The three through bores are Ø4.5 mm. The return bores are 10 mm apart. At the free end of the return the nominal Ø9 mm washer envelope leaves a 1.5 mm margin.
