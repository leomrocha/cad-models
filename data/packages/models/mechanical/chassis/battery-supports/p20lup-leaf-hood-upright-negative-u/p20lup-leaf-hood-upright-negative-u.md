## Geometry

Leaf terminal cover upright, negative u side. The proposed 3 mm formed support strip is 20 mm wide. Two Ø4.5 mm holes pass through the foot and two pass through the bridge mounting leg.

Local CAD axes X = v (height), Y = n (normal to the module face), Z = u offset from the strip centre.
The bend uses R3 mm inside and R6 mm outside radii. The two foot bores have n = 163 mm.
The bridge bores at v = 10 and 194 mm sit at u offset 5 mm.
