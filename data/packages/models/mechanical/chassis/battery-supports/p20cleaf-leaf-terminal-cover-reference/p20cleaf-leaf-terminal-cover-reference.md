## Geometry

Leaf six-module terminal hood at the reference terminal plane n = 162 mm. The proposed 3 mm polymer roof and walls have four 20 × 20 mm mounting ears with Ø4.5 mm bores.

Part-local millimetres. CAD X is u, Y is v and Z is n. The open rim is at n = 150 mm.
The inner cavity is 116 × 224 mm and the roof underside is n = 172 mm. The cable walls are intact. Installed terminal and harness positions set the final roof plane and cable openings.
