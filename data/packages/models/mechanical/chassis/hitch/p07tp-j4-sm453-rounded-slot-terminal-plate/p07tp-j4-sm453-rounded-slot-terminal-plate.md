# J4 SM453 terminal plate

**Catalogue draft ID** `p07tp-j4-sm453-rounded-slot-terminal-plate`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p07tp-j4-sm453-rounded-slot-terminal-plate.dwg), SHA-256 `129401734961e00bc03373e323c517b571765a70c5d0437a2a328fde5716f0c0`.

## Geometry

160 × 160 × 10 mm terminal plate with a 25.5 × 22.5 mm through-slot for the SM453 stem.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. XY origin at centre and Z=0 at bottom.

The approval report supplies the nominal slot dimensions. The plate outside dimensions are proposed.
