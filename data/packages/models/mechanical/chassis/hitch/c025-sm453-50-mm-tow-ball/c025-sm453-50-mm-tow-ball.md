## Geometry

SM453 tow ball body reconstructed from the 2017 approval drawing. The nominal Ø50 mm ball has a Ø18 mm top flat. The flange is Ø45 mm, and the nominal M22 × 1.5 stem extends 29.5 mm below the flange seat.

Millimetres. Z is the ball and stem axis. The flange underside is Z = 0 mm.
The dimensioned overall height is 109 mm. The calculated ball centre is 56.176192 mm above the flange seat.
The stem uses a nominal Ø22 mm cylindrical thread envelope. The unlabelled end-chamfer angle is interpreted as 45 degrees. The drawing body, cap nut and washer are separate components.
