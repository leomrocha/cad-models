# Caster primary pin flat split keeper

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

Flat split keeper for a Ø4 mm caster pin cross-bore. The model shows the legs spread in the retained position.

Origin at the mating pin cross-bore centre. X is insertion direction, Y is split-leg spread and Z is plate thickness. Millimetres.

The mating model is C018. The 1 mm thick keeper uses a 3.4 mm wide straight insertion section. Its nominal diametral insertion clearance is 0.456 mm. The retained tail is 8.4 mm wide. The start of the spread is 2 mm past the nominal shank surface.
