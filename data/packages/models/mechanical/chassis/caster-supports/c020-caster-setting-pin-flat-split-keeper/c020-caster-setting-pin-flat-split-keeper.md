# Caster setting pin flat split keeper

**Catalogue draft ID** `c020-caster-setting-pin-flat-split-keeper`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](c020-caster-setting-pin-flat-split-keeper.dwg), SHA-256 `e4978119ee4de080f065f3a8981ab6c02c8221860eba0529e8914e6f8148a25f`.

## Geometry

Flat split keeper for a Ø3 mm caster pin cross-bore. The model shows the legs spread in the retained position.

Origin at the mating pin cross-bore centre. X is insertion direction, Y is split-leg spread and Z is plate thickness. Millimetres.

The mating model is C019. The 1 mm thick keeper uses a 2.4 mm wide straight insertion section. Its nominal diametral insertion clearance is 0.400 mm. The retained tail is 7.4 mm wide. The start of the spread is 2 mm past the nominal shank surface.
