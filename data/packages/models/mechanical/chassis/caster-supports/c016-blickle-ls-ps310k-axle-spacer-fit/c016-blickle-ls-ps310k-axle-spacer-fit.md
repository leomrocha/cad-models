# Blickle LS-PS310K axle spacer fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-axle-spacer-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-axle-spacer-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `6efd3283504bc8a6adcaea9a9d64e36a6b4daaa84ea1f38cd96ff1212433be0e`.

## Geometry

Proposed Ø32 mm hub spacer with a Ø20.4 mm bore; two mirrored occurrences per caster.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø300 × 100 mm wheel, 355 mm total height and 85 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

One spacer is 15.5 mm thick and seats between the hub and the fork on each side.
