**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

4.5 mm triangular caster brace with a radius-16 mm primary-spacer relief.

Tractor source datums in millimetres. Positive Y is the left brace. Primary pivot and relief run along Y.

The nominal brace-to-cheek face gap is 0.5 mm. The primary spacer has 1 mm radial clearance.
