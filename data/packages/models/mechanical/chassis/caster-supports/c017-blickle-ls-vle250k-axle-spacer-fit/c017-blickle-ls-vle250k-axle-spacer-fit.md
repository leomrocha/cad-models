# Blickle LS-VLE250K axle spacer fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-axle-spacer-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-axle-spacer-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `1460064446423755fa021150dd38627d630e6a8fd8f484b2094aa7a65fa65a97`.

## Geometry

Proposed Ø32 mm hub spacer with a Ø20.4 mm bore; two mirrored occurrences per caster.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø250 × 85 mm wheel, 305 mm total height and 75 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

One spacer is 8.0 mm thick and seats between the hub and the fork on each side.
