# Blickle caster M20 axle retaining nut fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `blickle-caster-m20-axle-retaining-nut-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](blickle-caster-m20-axle-retaining-nut-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `607180a3e0fa69215f9458dfae02753b3bea210a02016ca2eaa2d07f0f8fc171`.

## Geometry

Proposed M20 retaining-nut envelope, 30 mm across flats and 12 mm high, shared by both caster sizes.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø250 × 85 mm wheel, 305 mm total height and 75 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

Proposed M20 retention envelope with 0.2 mm radial modelling clearance.
The local origin is the bearing face on the axle axis.
