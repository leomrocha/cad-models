# Blickle LS-PS310K wheel body fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-wheel-body-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-wheel-body-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `3d6672e43acb77df4f0887ca32a4ed2ec82c2daa5ed03dc647f6dd8198e8ee8c`.

## Geometry

Exterior wheel fit solid with a Ø20 mm bore and 75 mm hub length.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø300 × 100 mm wheel, 355 mm total height and 85 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

The PS 310/20-75K wheel-family candidate supplies the bore and hub length. The tread and rim contours are proposed exterior fit geometry.
