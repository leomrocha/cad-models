## Geometry

85 × 340 × 18 mm upper clevis tie with two 8 mm integral feet for the J4 caster support.

Part-local millimetres. X is transverse, Y is up from the underside of the bearing feet, and Z is forward from the rear face.

The web is 10 mm thick. The integral bearing feet seat on Y-up datum zero over 680 mm² per side. Their transverse bands are X = −38 to −30 mm and X = 30 to 38 mm. One part definition serves the 250 mm and 300 mm caster variants.
