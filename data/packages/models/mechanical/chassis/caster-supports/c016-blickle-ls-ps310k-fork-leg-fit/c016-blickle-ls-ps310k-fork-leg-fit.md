# Blickle LS-PS310K fork leg fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-fork-leg-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-fork-leg-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `773ab797997c06b8850beb1fb38e1698870a5e83ac33fca90b87696b966de86c`.

## Geometry

6 mm proposed single fork leg, with Ø20.4 mm axle bore on the nominal axle axis.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø300 × 100 mm wheel, 355 mm total height and 85 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

Use one left occurrence and one reflected right occurrence. The nominal wheel side gap is 3 mm per side.
