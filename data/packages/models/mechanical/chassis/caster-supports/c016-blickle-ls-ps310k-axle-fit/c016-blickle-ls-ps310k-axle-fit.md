# Blickle LS-PS310K axle fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-axle-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-axle-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `d9f9b1dd79d9206f398f86ecffa01b0c19074f6790b8b62ce8f9ac83b2c62094`.

## Geometry

Proposed Ø20 mm axle with integral head and a 16 mm retention zone.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø300 × 100 mm wheel, 355 mm total height and 85 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

The grip through both fork legs is 118.0 mm. Length under the head is 134.0 mm. The retention end is a smooth major-diameter representation.
