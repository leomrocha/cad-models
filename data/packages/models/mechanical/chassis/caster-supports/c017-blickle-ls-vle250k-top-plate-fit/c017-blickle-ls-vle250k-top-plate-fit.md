# Blickle LS-VLE250K top mounting plate fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-top-plate-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-top-plate-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `6d150356b97dad62ab317115a81c98e5fc815ec175998f0acffc4534150fc033`.

## Geometry

Manufacturer footprint and four Ø14 mm bores, with an 8 mm proposed plate thickness.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø250 × 85 mm wheel, 305 mm total height and 75 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

The manufacturer plate footprint is 175 × 140 mm. The four bores use the 140 × 105 mm manufacturer grid.
