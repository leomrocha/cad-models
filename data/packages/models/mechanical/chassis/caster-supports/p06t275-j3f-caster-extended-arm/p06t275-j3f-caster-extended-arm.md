# J3f caster arm with 70 mm rear extension

**Catalogue draft ID** `p06t275-j3f-caster-extended-arm`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p06t275-j3f-caster-extended-arm.dwg), SHA-256 `283f876b0514819d98ef0c49a8073f83d0df308fa8d14b46df80bf769072493d`.

## Geometry

275 mm caster arm with 50 × 40 × 3 mm box section and two retained-datum transverse sleeve axes.

Part-local millimetres. X is width, Y is section height and Z runs forward from the rear end.

The 70 mm extension is at the rear. The Ø30 mm pivot-sleeve and Ø22 mm setting-sleeve axes lie 150 mm and 250 mm from the rear, both 2 mm below the tube centre. Their installed axes remain at the original frame coordinates.
