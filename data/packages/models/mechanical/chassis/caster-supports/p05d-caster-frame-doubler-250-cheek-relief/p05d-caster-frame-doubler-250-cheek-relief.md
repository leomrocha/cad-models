# Caster frame doubler with 250 mm clevis cheek relief

**Catalogue draft ID** `p05d-caster-frame-doubler-250-cheek-relief`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal for the 250 mm caster.

[Native DWG 2018](p05d-caster-frame-doubler-250-cheek-relief.dwg), SHA-256 `b9c087ae752478b64f275ac9b180acd1bad679aa4de667a52d786f271e6bcd47`.

## Geometry

10 mm frame doubler with two open 33 × 9 mm clevis reliefs and four retained Ø13 mm mounting bores.

Tractor installation frame in millimetres. X points forward, Y left and Z up.

The reliefs give 0.5 mm nominal clearance to the cheek on each machined side and at the rear of each relief. The reliefs open on the forward edge. The existing four bolt axes and outside size are preserved. The nominal remaining web seating area is 178.75 mm² per side.
