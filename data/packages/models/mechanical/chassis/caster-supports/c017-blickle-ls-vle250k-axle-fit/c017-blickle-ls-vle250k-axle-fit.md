# Blickle LS-VLE250K axle fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-axle-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-axle-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `8c82d4134fa947388ddc41580de51c918e86f06a94d44041ed837c45a4cd3c2c`.

## Geometry

Proposed Ø20 mm axle with integral head and a 16 mm retention zone.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø250 × 85 mm wheel, 305 mm total height and 75 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

The grip through both fork legs is 103.0 mm. Length under the head is 119.0 mm. The retention end is a smooth major-diameter representation.
