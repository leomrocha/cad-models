# J3f caster arm with 60 mm rear extension

**Catalogue draft ID** `p06t265-j3f-caster-extended-arm`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p06t265-j3f-caster-extended-arm.dwg), SHA-256 `346888683b887ada5d85369a54ac6c6a012a31bbcd0847bfb7a82c4d0549b61e`.

## Geometry

265 mm caster arm with 50 × 40 × 3 mm tube section and two retained-datum transverse sleeve axes.

Part-local millimetres. X is width, Y is section height and Z runs forward from the rear end.

The 60 mm extension is at the rear. The Ø30 mm pivot-sleeve and Ø22 mm setting-sleeve axes lie 140 mm and 240 mm from the rear, both 2 mm below the tube centre. Their installed axes remain at the original frame coordinates.
