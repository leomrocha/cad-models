# Blickle LS-PS310K top mounting plate fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-top-plate-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-top-plate-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `7ccb539b926802cecd76ab9e3725e96e441f66d65f8f82fd92faf45bb1e9f372`.

## Geometry

Manufacturer footprint and four Ø14 mm bores, with an 8 mm proposed plate thickness.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø300 × 100 mm wheel, 355 mm total height and 85 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

The manufacturer plate footprint is 200 × 160 mm. The four bores use the 160 × 120 mm manufacturer grid.
