# Blickle LS-VLE250K swivel body fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-swivel-body-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-swivel-body-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `a98212581e5fb33fb39f314a5fd663d05cd4b657325643c3eb129e437aa050fd`.

## Geometry

Stepped 26 mm proposed exterior envelope between the fork and mounting plate.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø250 × 85 mm wheel, 305 mm total height and 75 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

The proposed crown is Ø132 mm and the collar is Ø118 mm.
