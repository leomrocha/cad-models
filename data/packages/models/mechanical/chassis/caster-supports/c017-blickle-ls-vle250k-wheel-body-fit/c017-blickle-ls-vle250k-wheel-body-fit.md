# Blickle LS-VLE250K wheel body fit proposal

**Catalogue draft ID** `c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-wheel-body-fit`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Proposed external fit geometry.

[Native DWG 2018](c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-wheel-body-fit.dwg), SHA-256 `6a2b5485aa29d7be8672b2357d7e81d9951d503d825fceb9a3912d6396796248`.

## Geometry

Exterior wheel fit solid with a Ø20 mm bore and 75 mm hub length.

Millimetres. Origin below the swivel axis on nominal ground. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The manufacturer row supplies a Ø250 × 85 mm wheel, 305 mm total height and 75 mm swivel offset.
The family illustration supplies the visible fork and swivel arrangement. Exterior levels, fork thickness, axle selection and the undimensioned contour are proposed fit inputs.

The VLE 250/20-75K wheel-family candidate supplies the bore and hub length. The tread and rim contours are proposed exterior fit geometry.
