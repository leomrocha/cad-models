# BMW J tray angle for battery tabs and removable shield uprights

**Catalogue draft ID** `p18ja-j-bmw-removable-shield-support-angle-battery-tabs`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p18ja-j-bmw-removable-shield-support-angle-battery-tabs.dwg), SHA-256 `80203870bb6a7472edfcb9f5940036d8634986784a583d506f0ec6a15766cc3f`.

## Geometry

440 mm formed tray support angle, 37 mm flange, 30 mm leg and 3 mm sheet. Two Ø8.5 mm battery-tab bores and two Ø4.5 mm shield-upright bores pass through the flange.
The battery-tab bore axes are X = −123.75 and 273.75 mm on Y = 139 mm.
The nominal minimum washer lands are 3 mm for a Ø16 mm washer and 3.5 mm for a Ø9 mm washer. The two washer envelopes at each side have 0.788 mm of nominal separation.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up.

The native bend radii are 3 and 6 mm. The two bores pass through Z = 572–575 mm on Y = 140 mm at X = −137 and 287 mm.
Install the left member in the saved position and mirror it for the right.
