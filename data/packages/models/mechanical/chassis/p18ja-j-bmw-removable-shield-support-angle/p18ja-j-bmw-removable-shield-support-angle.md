# BMW J tray support angle for removable shield uprights

**Catalogue draft ID** `p18ja-j-bmw-removable-shield-support-angle`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p18ja-j-bmw-removable-shield-support-angle.dwg), SHA-256 `86409ee577171aa4e3f5074e7285aa84fdd07f729bd6149c4dcda45059728fdb`.

## Geometry

440 mm cut of nominal 30 × 30 × 3 mm tray support angle with two Ø4.5 mm bores matching the BMW shield upright fasteners.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up.

The native bend radii are 3 and 6 mm. The two bores pass through Z = 572–575 mm on Y = 140 mm at X = −137 and 287 mm. A Ø9 mm washer has 0.5 mm minimum nominal land at the free flange edge and 3.5 mm to either end.
Install the left member in the saved position and mirror it for the right.
