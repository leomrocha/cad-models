## Geometry

61 mm square-ended edge protector for the tractor splash-panel installation. The single solid represents the estimated installed outline of EMKA 1010-12.

Millimetres. X across the sheet. Y from the trim mouth toward the crown. Z along the cut.

The manufacturer drawing gives 8.3 mm width, 11.5 mm height, 2.4 mm crown wall and a 0.8–2.5 mm edge grip range.
The outline between dimensioned features is traced from the drawing and represented by straight segments.
The installed slot is modelled around a centred, fully seated 1.5 mm sheet.
The single solid represents the installed profile envelope. The source material is black EPDM, 60 ± 5 Shore A, with a steel spring core.
Installed quantity per splash-panel pair is 2.
