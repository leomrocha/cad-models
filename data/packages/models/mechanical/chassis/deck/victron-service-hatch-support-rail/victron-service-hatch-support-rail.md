# Victron service hatch removable support rail

**Catalogue draft ID** `victron-service-hatch-support-rail`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](victron-service-hatch-support-rail.dwg), SHA-256 `859c8f94df7fb7d3a59508bc78b1dbde1d33f634923e22b01565e6985ef8d983`.

## Geometry

330 × 30 × 6 mm removable hatch rail with six M6 × 1 nominal thread references.

Part-local millimetres, Z = 0 on the underside. Rail XY is centred. Deck and cover XY share the frame datum.

The cylinders represent the M6 major diameter. Installed quantities use two rails per hatch.
