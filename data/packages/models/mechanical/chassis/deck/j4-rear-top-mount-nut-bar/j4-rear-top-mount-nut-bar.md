# J4 rear top-mount two-hole nut bar

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-rear-top-mount-nut-bar`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j4-rear-top-mount-nut-bar.dwg), SHA-256 `0f66b09bf930edadf56fe0d643a5993906453c43beb2d75d59df8239b1fcebc1`.

## Geometry

50 × 24 × 33 mm internal J4 nut bar with two nominal M10 blind thread envelopes.

Millimetres. X along the rail, Y up in the part section, +Z inward from the screw-entry face.

The two nominal Ø10 mm cut envelopes are 25 mm deep, spaced 30 mm apart, with 8 mm of remaining stock. The cylinders represent nominal thread clearance.
The proposed 33 mm depth leaves 1 mm of nominal insertion space in the 34 mm rail cavity.
