## Geometry

Steel deck upright bridge cap, 60 × 70 × 3 mm. The 70 mm direction extends the upright contact plane outboard to the ledge.

Part-local millimetres. Origin is the upright section centre on the cap underside. Positive Y points to the outer ledge on the left side.
The rectangular outline spans X = −30 to +30 mm and Y = −20 to +50 mm. The underside is at Z = 0 mm and the upper contact face is at Z = 3 mm.
