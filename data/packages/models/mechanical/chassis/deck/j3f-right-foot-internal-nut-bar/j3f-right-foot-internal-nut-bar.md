## Geometry

50 × 24 × 34 mm internal nut bar with two nominal M10 blind thread envelopes.

Millimetres. X along the rail, Y up in the part section, +Z inward from the screw-entry face.

The two nominal Ø10 mm cut envelopes are 25 mm deep, spaced 30 mm apart, with 9 mm of remaining stock. The cylinders represent nominal thread clearance.
