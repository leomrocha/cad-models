600 × 400 × 3 mm fixed deck with a 370 × 264 mm hatch opening and twelve Ø6.5 mm fixing bores.

Part-local millimetres, Z = 0 on the underside. Rail XY is centred. Deck and cover XY share the frame datum.

The open bore axes match the proposed hatch rail pattern.
