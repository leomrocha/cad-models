## Geometry

Flat 600 × 25 × 3 mm deck ledge with three Ø6.5 mm through bores.

Part-local millimetres. XY is centred on the strip and Z starts at its underside.

Bore centres are X = −210, 0 and 210 mm along the ledge centreline. Use two ledges per deck.
