## Geometry

J4 rear top-mount foot for the deck upright and lifting stanchion.

Millimetres. Local X along the rail, Y outboard on the left and Z up from the seat.

The proposed steel plate measures 60 × 57 × 8 mm. Native measurements confirmed both Ø11 mm through bores at local (X, Y) = (15, 12) and (45, 12) mm.

The wide end supports the original upright position. The inboard seat mounts to the top rail wall. The load path through the plate and fasteners requires separate structural checks.
