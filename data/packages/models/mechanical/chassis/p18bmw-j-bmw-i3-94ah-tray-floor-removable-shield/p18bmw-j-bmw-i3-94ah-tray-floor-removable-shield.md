## Geometry

Raised J tray floor with four 8 × 20 mm extensions, four Ø8.5 mm battery-tab holes and four Ø4.5 mm shield-upright holes. The floor is 3 mm thick.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. The proposed module centre is X = 75 mm.

The hole pattern transfers the photo-derived mounting features of the saved battery reconstruction.

The upright axes are X = −137 and 287 mm, Y = ±140 mm. Each 20 mm extension provides at least 5.5 mm nominal edge land outside a Ø9 mm washer. The mating support angle lies on the same axes and requires through-clearance for this joint.
