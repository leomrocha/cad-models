# J3f detachable ledge cap with eccentric M8 receiver

**Catalogue draft ID** `p04jcp-j3f-detachable-ledge-threaded-cap`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p04jcp-j3f-detachable-ledge-threaded-cap.dwg), SHA-256 `d2ca86459eb8a661cff501d3cd42f6bee1f18e99df21a635c186dab8e718170d`.

## Geometry

50 × 30 × 3 mm deck upright cap with one eccentric 8 mm-deep M8 receiver.

Millimetres. Local origin on the post centre at the cap underside; X runs toward the ledge end.

The receiver axis is X = 18 mm, Y = 2.5 mm from the upright centre. The lower conical seat meets the ledge through a Ø12.25 mm interface at the cap top.
