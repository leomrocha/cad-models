## Geometry

0.25 mm dielectric contact-layer geometry for the reconstructed BMW i3 94 Ah module. The 385 × 310 mm supported base, four tabs and four Ø8.5 mm holes follow the saved case.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Module centre X = 75 mm.

The layer spans Z = 578–578.25 mm in the raised tray arrangement. Each tab is 32 mm wide and extends 12.5 mm beyond the case body.
The outline and hole centres are based on the photo-derived module reconstruction.
