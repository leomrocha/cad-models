## Geometry

J4 shaft retainer washer with Ø40 mm outside diameter, Ø9 mm bore and 7 mm thickness. The nominal 90° screw seat is Ø18.25 mm at the opening and 4.625 mm deep.

Millimetres. Shaft axis is local Z. Face Z = 0 faces the screw head.
