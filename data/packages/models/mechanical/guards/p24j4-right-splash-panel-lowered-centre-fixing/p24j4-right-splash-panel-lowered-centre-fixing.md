## Geometry

J4 right splash sheet with two Ø16 mm controller fastener reliefs. The 159 × 45 mm open-top notch clears the modeled crossing cable envelope. A Ø6.5 mm hole at X = 0 mm and Z = 694 mm aligns with the lowered centre crossbar.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. The cutting datum is at positive Y for both parts.
Sheet thickness 1.5 mm. The two relief axes are at X = −137 and 77 mm, Z = 658 mm.
5 original Ø6.5 mm mounting holes remain.

Install this pattern with Y mirrored. The top notch replaces the former central upper fixing.
The notch spans X = −48 to 111 mm and Z = 705 to 750 mm.

The lowered fixing leaves 7.75 mm of sheet between the hole rim and the notch floor.
The native solid has both Ø6.5 mm fixing rims, five inherited mounting holes and both Ø16 mm clearance bores.
