J4 outboard splash fixing spacer. Ø12 mm outside diameter, Ø6.6 mm bore and 5.5 mm nominal length.

Millimetres. Tube axis is local +Z. Panel-facing plane is local Z = 0.
The proposed measured length range is 5.4–5.6 mm. Twelve spacers serve the paired panels.
