**Catalogue draft ID** `p20cbmw-bmw-terminal-shield-nlive150`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal for the nominal 150 mm live-height reference.

## Geometry

Removable BMW module shield for the nominal 150 mm live-height reference. It has a 397 × 322 mm roof, four 20 × 20 mm ears and a cable opening through one skirt.

Millimetres in the reconstructed module datum. X along case length, Y across the case, Z up from the module base.

The roof spans Z = 160–163 mm. The 3 mm skirts end at Z = 137 mm. Four Ø4.5 mm ear holes use the saved independent upright datum.
The opening extends through the negative-Y skirt and follows a conservative projection of the reconstructed cable route with a 17 mm planning clearance diameter.
