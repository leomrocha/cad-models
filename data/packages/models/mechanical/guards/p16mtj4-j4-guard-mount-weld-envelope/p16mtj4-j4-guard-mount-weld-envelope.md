## Geometry

Triangular 1 mm-leg nominal weld envelope for the J4 upper guard mounting flange.

Millimetres. X along the weld, Y positive left, Z up.

The 30 mm long triangular solid has a 1 mm leg on each mating face. Its nominal geometric volume is 15 mm³.
