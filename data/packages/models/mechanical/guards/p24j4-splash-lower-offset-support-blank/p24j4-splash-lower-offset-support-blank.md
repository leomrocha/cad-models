## Geometry

J4 splash-panel lower offset support machining blank.

Millimetres. X is relative to the fixing axis. Y and Z use the left chassis datums.
The 3.5 mm rail stem spans local X = -8 to -2 mm.
The 8 mm boss sits against the panel at Y = 173.5 mm. The Ø5 mm through pilot is normal to Y at local X = 0 mm and Z = 570 mm.
The pilot is the proposed input for M6 tapping.
