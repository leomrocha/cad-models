## Geometry

J4 left splash sheet with two Ø16 mm controller fastener reliefs.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. The cutting datum is at positive Y for both parts.
Sheet thickness 1.5 mm. The two relief axes are at X = −137 and 77 mm, Z = 658 mm.
6 original Ø6.5 mm mounting holes remain.
