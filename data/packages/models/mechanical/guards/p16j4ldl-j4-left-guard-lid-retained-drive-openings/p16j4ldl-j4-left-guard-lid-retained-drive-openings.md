# J4 left guard lid with retained drive openings

Revision 1. Discussion retained-drive machining reference for the J4 rectangular chain guard. Millimetres. Local Z is normal to the sheet.

1. **Sheet.** 317.524792 × 420.798686 × 1.5 mm proposed steel.
2. **Fixing holes.** 10 through holes, Ø6.5 mm, on the A05 lid mounting pattern. Native readback checked each axis and both sheet-face trims.
3. **Drive openings.** A Ø54 mm opening at the wheel axis clears the checked Ø50 mm hub blank with 2 mm nominal radial space.
4. **Scope.** The openings cover the retained hub blank and, on the back sheet, the modeled motor shaft and nominal key. Fit of the complete guard requires the assembled sprocket and its source mounting geometry.
5. **DWG SHA-256.** `bb53c7b537e2c90c77bf2632aeca0574735644524ab7beeba042a42ea7bbe5f1`.
