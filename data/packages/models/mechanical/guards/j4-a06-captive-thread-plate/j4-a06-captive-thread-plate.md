# J4 A06 captive threaded plate

Revision 1. Discussion construction proposal. Millimetres. Local XY follows the plate face.

1. **Plate.** 18 × 11.25 × 6 mm, located on the inboard face of the A06 support leg.
2. **Thread envelope.** The Ø6 mm cylindrical bore represents the nominal M6 × 1 thread major diameter. The model represents 6 mm of axial thread length.
3. **Assembly.** The fixed plate allows the guard to be fastened from the outside. The proposal requires separate weld, thread engagement and screw locking checks.
