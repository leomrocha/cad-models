# J4 right guard back sheet with retained drive openings

Revision 1. Discussion retained-drive machining reference for the J4 rectangular chain guard. Millimetres. Local Z is normal to the sheet.

1. **Sheet.** 359.798686 × 402.298686 × 1.5 mm proposed steel.
2. **Fixing holes.** 4 through holes, Ø6.5 mm, on the A06 stationary mounting pattern. Native readback checked each axis and both sheet-face trims.
3. **Drive openings.** A Ø54 mm opening at the wheel axis clears the checked Ø50 mm hub blank with 2 mm nominal radial space. The back sheet has a 31 × 61 mm rounded slot at the motor shaft. Its centre travels from Z = 415 to 445 mm.
4. **Scope.** The openings cover the retained hub blank and, on the back sheet, the modeled motor shaft and nominal key. Fit of the complete guard requires the assembled sprocket and its source mounting geometry.
5. **DWG SHA-256.** `904b08e7e217190aca35111a0f240e451846decbdc7283b8a11d774af3d35129`.
