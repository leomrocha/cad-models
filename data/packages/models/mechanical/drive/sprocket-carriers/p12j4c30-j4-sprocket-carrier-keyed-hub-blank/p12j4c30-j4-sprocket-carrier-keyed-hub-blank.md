## Geometry

Nominal keyed cylindrical blank for a J4 sprocket carrier with a Ø30 mm bore.

Millimetres. +Z runs outboard from the hub inner face. The keyway opens toward +Y.

Ø50 × 40 mm cylinder, Ø30 mm axial through bore and an 8 mm wide through-keyway.
The keyway floor is 3.3 mm beyond the nominal bore radius. The nominal gap above the seated key is 0.3 mm.
The left-side reference station spans Y = 132.2–172.2 mm with a 56.8 mm spacer beyond its outboard face.
