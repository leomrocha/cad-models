## Geometry

J4 parking-brake mounting upstand. The proposed steel plate measures 50 × 60 × 8 mm with four Ø9 mm through holes at 25 × 35 mm centres.

Part-local millimetres. X runs along the 50 mm edge, Y runs along the 60 mm edge and Z is the thickness. The wing contact face is local Z = 0 mm and the bridge contact edge is local Y = 0 mm.
Hole centres are X = 12.5 and 37.5 mm at Y = 12.5 and 47.5 mm. The minimum nominal hole-edge ligament is 8 mm.
Equipment placement uses the caliper mounting plane and disc keepout for the selected shaft arrangement.
