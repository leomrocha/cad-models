# J4 30 mm wheel shaft with two keyways

**Catalogue draft ID** `p12j430-j4-30mm-keyed-wheel-shaft`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion machining proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p12j430-j4-30mm-keyed-wheel-shaft.dwg), SHA-256 `b180072c7d02c3f654f0178b722b2ae3e9802a270f4d89a00dc170d70b688ab4`.

## Geometry

J4 Ø30 × 279 mm wheel shaft with two nominal 8 × 40 mm round-ended keyways and retained axial drill features.

Millimetres. +Z points outboard from the shaft inner face. Keyways open in +Y.

The two slots have 4 mm radial depth and R4 ends. Axial spans from the inner face are [[122.2, 162.2], [219, 259]] mm. The outer thread envelope and wheel pocket meet over 5 mm axially, with 6 mm nominal radial web. Joint fit and shaft strength use separate checks.
