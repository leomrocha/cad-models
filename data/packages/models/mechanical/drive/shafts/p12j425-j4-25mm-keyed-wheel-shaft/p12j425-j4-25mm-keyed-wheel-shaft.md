# J4 25 mm wheel shaft with two keyways

**Catalogue draft ID** `p12j425-j4-25mm-keyed-wheel-shaft`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion machining proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p12j425-j4-25mm-keyed-wheel-shaft.dwg), SHA-256 `335b51ad6f0080847c2ab14b01d1475ccde0d3d179f43211a951950a9bd4d7b7`.

## Geometry

J4 Ø25 × 279 mm wheel shaft with two nominal 8 × 40 mm round-ended keyways and retained axial drill features.

Millimetres. +Z points outboard from the shaft inner face. Keyways open in +Y.

The two slots have 4 mm radial depth and R4 ends. Axial spans from the inner face are [[119.8, 159.8], [219, 259]] mm. The outer thread envelope and wheel pocket meet over 5 mm axially, with 3.5 mm nominal radial web. Joint fit and shaft strength use separate checks.
