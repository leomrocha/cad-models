## Geometry

J4 nominal Ø25 × 279 mm wheel shaft with a finite inner drilling profile and nominal outer thread envelope.

Millimetres. +Z runs from the inner shaft face toward the wheel. The left installed faces are Y = 10 and 289 mm.

The Ø6.8 mm inner drill has 25 mm cylindrical depth and a 118° included point. The outer Ø10 × 25 mm cavity represents the nominal M10 × 1.5 envelope. Keyways, entry chamfers and thread forms have separate machining inputs.
