# J4 Knott wheel flange for 25 mm shaft

**Design status** Discussion machining proposal.

## Geometry

J4 flange with a keyed nominal Ø25 mm bore, Knott 4 × 100 mm wheel pattern and recessed outer washer fit.

Millimetres. +Z runs outboard. The hub starts at Z = 0 and the rim sits at Z = 60.

The proposed Ø59.8 mm pilot has a 4 mm projection. Its Ø51 mm inner pocket accepts the nominal Ø50 mm end washer. The bore has an 8 mm wide broached keyway with 3.3 mm nominal depth. The four Ø12 cylinders represent M12 × 1.5 thread envelopes.
