# J4 relieved outer wheel retainer washer

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-relieved-outer-wheel-retainer-washer`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion machining proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j4-relieved-outer-wheel-retainer-washer.dwg), SHA-256 `a74d76d8da83aa2d72da19684364e8440a206519ff8d240e9c7a77923eeb82da`.

## Geometry

J4 Ø50 × 5 mm retaining washer with a Ø30.4 mm underside relief 0.5 mm deep.

Millimetres. +Z points outboard. Z = 0 is the flange bearing face.

The Ø10.5 mm through bore is retained. The relief leaves 4.5 mm centre thickness and a nominal 1,237.662 mm² annulus on the flange bearing face.
