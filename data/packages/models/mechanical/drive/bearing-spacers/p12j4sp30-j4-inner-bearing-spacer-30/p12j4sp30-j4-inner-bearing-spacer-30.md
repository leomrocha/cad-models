## Geometry

J4 inner bearing spacer with Ø40 mm outside diameter, Ø30.2 mm bore and 4.1 mm axial length.

Millimetres. Shaft axis is local Z. Face Z = 0 contacts the inner retainer washer.

Left installation contact stations Y = 10 to 14.1 mm. Mirror on the right.
The saved solid represents the nominal plain annulus. Insert bearing-face and chamfer geometry govern the contact check.
