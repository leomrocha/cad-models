## Geometry

J4 25 mm branch lower bearing cantilever. The steel part measures 258 × 46 × 25 mm with two Ø11 mm through bores. The 46 mm width supports the left outer hanger post at nominal Y = 99 mm while the bearing fixings stay at Y = 110 mm.

Millimetres in the left-inner bearing station. X along the rail, Y along the shaft, Z up.
The underside contact plane is Z = 275.5 mm.
[Original lower bearing cantilever](../p13j425bar-j4-solid-lower-cantilever-25-branch/p13j425bar-j4-solid-lower-cantilever-25-branch.md), SHA-256 `e3ba18ca3b92c413330bcb524864185032b5627fcf464e10a8efe7b70d4f7bf7`.

The installed bar spans Y = 84–130 mm. The post spans Y = 84–114 mm.
The minimum nominal transverse edge land at the mounting opening is 14.5 mm.
