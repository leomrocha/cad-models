## Geometry

J4 30 mm branch lower bearing cantilever. The steel part measures 258 × 46 × 25 mm with two Ø14 mm through bores. The 46 mm width supports the left outer hanger post at nominal Y = 99 mm while the bearing fixings stay at Y = 110 mm. The two proposed 90° seats have an Ø26.88 mm mouth and 6.44 mm depth.

Millimetres in the left-inner bearing station. X along the rail, Y along the shaft, Z up.
The underside contact plane is Z = 281.9 mm.
[Original lower bearing cantilever](../p13j4bar-j4-solid-lower-bearing-cantilever/p13j4bar-j4-solid-lower-bearing-cantilever.md), SHA-256 `1b3eed939178a6258965940e201397a0968a3342c9c349ce4eb022a4458db7a2`.

The installed bar spans Y = 84–130 mm. The post spans Y = 84–114 mm.
The minimum nominal transverse edge land at the mounting opening is 6.56 mm.
