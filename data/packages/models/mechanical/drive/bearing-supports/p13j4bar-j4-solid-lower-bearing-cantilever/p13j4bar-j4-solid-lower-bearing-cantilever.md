## Geometry

J4 30 mm branch lower bearing cantilever. The steel bar measures 258 × 40 × 25 mm with two Ø14 mm through bores. The two proposed 90° seats have an Ø26.88 mm mouth and 6.44 mm depth.

Millimetres in the left-inner bearing station. X along the rail, Y along the shaft, Z up.
The underside contact plane is Z = 281.9 mm.
