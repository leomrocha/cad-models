## Geometry

J4 20 mm branch inverted bearing support plate. The steel part measures 150 × 40 × 6 mm with two Ø11 mm through bores.

Millimetres in the left-inner bearing station. X along the rail, Y along the shaft, Z up.
The underside contact plane is Z = 266.3 mm.
