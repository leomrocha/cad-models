## Geometry and installation

Millimetres. Catalogue model-257 panel datum. XY is outer panel mounting plane at Z=0. +Z points toward red actuator and outside the panel.

- The panel engagement barrel uses the Ø22 mm source datum with 2–4 mm proposed receiving panel thickness. Photo-derived actuator and rear-body estimates, a nominal hex retainer and six source-visible terminal envelopes are separate solids.
- Actuator access is in +Z, proposed Ø45 mm for 18–30 mm. The spade mates approach from −Z for a proposed 10–20 mm depth.
