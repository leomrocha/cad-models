## Geometry and installation

Millimetres. Own accessory local frame, body underside at Z=0. +X toward the battery mating port.

- The compact standalone trial case is 52 × 25 × 16 mm. The design envelope ranges are 43–70 mm length, 19–32 mm width and 12–23 mm height. The Hiwonder 12.6 V bundle charger is separately identified.
- The USB-A plug approaches from −X with 12–20 mm service travel. The proposed four-way balance interface approaches from +X with 12–25 mm service travel. This mechanical accessory view has its own coordinate frame.
