## Geometry and installation

Millimetres. Model 315. Rear collar reference XY at Z=0, +Z along antenna toward tip, origin on RF axis.

- The January 2023 revision 5.53 drawing specifies the 58.6 mm body length, Ø25.0 mm main barrel, Ø27.3 mm collar and 16.1 mm collar span, each ±0.3 mm.
- The retained silhouette stations define the taper and tip. The nominal centre recess is Ø7.583 mm and 2 mm deep, with proposed depth 1–3 mm. The rear seal spans 0.4 mm. The collar and SMA-J are coaxial.
- SMA-J coupling approaches the rear from −Z. The proposed reserve is Ø12 mm for 15–25 mm axially from the rear land.
