## Geometry

Nominal SKF mounting datum and catalogue envelope 130 × 36 × 70.5 mm. Two 19.5 × 11.5 mm oblong slots have 102 mm centre spacing. The shaft axis is 36.5 mm above the mounting plane.

Millimetres. Mount plane Z = 0. Local X along the line joining the two mounting slots. Local Y along the shaft. Positive Y is the assigned locking-device side.
The unit uses housing SY 505 M and insert YAR 205-2F.
The Ø25 mm bore datums are at the two insert end planes.
The source inner-ring width is 34.1 mm.
The 2020 product-sheet height is 70 mm. The model uses the 2013 catalogue envelope.
The drawing consists of an envelope and interface datums. Detailed casting and seal geometry require an insert-specific section.
