## Geometry

Nominal SKF mounting datum and catalogue envelope 152 × 40 × 82.5 mm. Two 23.5 × 14 mm oblong slots have 117.5 mm centre spacing. The shaft axis is 42.9 mm above the mounting plane.

Millimetres. Mount plane Z = 0. Local X along the line joining the two mounting slots. Local Y along the shaft. Positive Y is the assigned locking-device side.
The unit uses housing SY 506 M and insert YAR 206-2F.
The Ø30 mm bore datums are at the two insert end planes.
The source inner-ring width is 38.1 mm.
The 2020 product-sheet height is 82 mm. The model uses the 2013 catalogue envelope.
The drawing consists of an envelope and interface datums. Detailed casting and seal geometry require an insert-specific section.
