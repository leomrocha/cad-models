## Geometry

Maximum manufacturer tolerance clearance solid for Trelleborg T539 18×8.50-8 6PR. The nominal outside diameter is 466 mm ±1.5% and the nominal width is 222 mm ±2%.

Local millimetres. The tyre axis is Z and the tyre centre is the origin.
Upper limits give Ø472.99 mm and 226.44 mm axial width. The solid occupies every point inside that cylinder for clearance screening. Bead geometry, tread and loaded deflection require their own source geometry.
