# Blickle LS-VLE250K wheel and mounting reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-dimensional-reference`. Revision 1.

[Native DWG 2018](c017-blickle-ls-vle250k-dimensional-reference.dwg), SHA-256 `2df72a3682959e79cb7edee202dfe7f6ed582980904bbd21884bebf5f15f171c`.

## Geometry

Manufacturer-dimensioned 250 × 85 mm wheel envelope and 140 × 105 mm mounting pattern with four Ø14 mm circles. The mount plane is 305 mm above ground.

Millimetres. Ground is Z = 0. The swivel-axis ground projection is the origin. The nominal axle follows Y and the trail points toward negative X.

Plate outline 175 × 140 mm. The centered outline and hole grid share a proposed placement datum.
The nominal offset is 75 mm. The circular plan datum gives a 204.466 mm calculated radius for the wheel envelope over a full swivel.
The geometry comprises the wheel envelope, four hole circles and the mounting and swivel datums.
