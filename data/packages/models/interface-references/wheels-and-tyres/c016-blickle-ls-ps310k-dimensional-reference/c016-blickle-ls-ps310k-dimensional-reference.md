# Blickle LS-PS310K wheel and mounting reference

**Catalogue draft ID** `c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-dimensional-reference`. Revision 1.

[Native DWG 2018](c016-blickle-ls-ps310k-dimensional-reference.dwg), SHA-256 `06ad2dd15aa2eca56be5988b31ccfe107c1c75164bca7440b16398585b5ce583`.

## Geometry

Manufacturer-dimensioned 300 × 100 mm wheel envelope and 160 × 120 mm mounting pattern with four Ø14 mm circles. The mount plane is 355 mm above ground.

Millimetres. Ground is Z = 0. The swivel-axis ground projection is the origin. The nominal axle follows Y and the trail points toward negative X.

Plate outline 200 × 160 mm. The centered outline and hole grid share a proposed placement datum.
The nominal offset is 85 mm. The circular plan datum gives a 240.260 mm calculated radius for the wheel envelope over a full swivel.
The geometry comprises the wheel envelope, four hole circles and the mounting and swivel datums.
