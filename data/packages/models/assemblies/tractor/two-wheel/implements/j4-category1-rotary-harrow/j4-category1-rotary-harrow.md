# Category 1 750 mm rotary harrow

**Representation.** Five timed vertical shafts, removable paired tines, a guarded gear-train layout and an indexed trailing depth roller.

1. **Native CAD.** [DWG 2018](j4-category1-rotary-harrow.dwg). SHA-256: 8b69d1a030e40c04c3557d1778f0e68ab46ae0cbbca814f6aacdc4cd118cd835.
2. **Native checks.** 112 individually named part records passed the native geometry checks.
3. **Working arrangement.** Nominal 750 mm span. The output shafts are on 150 mm centres. The five gear bodies represent pitch layout stock. The tine phasing and 18 mm nominal guard gap are analytical fit results.
4. **Hitch.** Category 1 with 683 mm shoulder span, 460 mm mast height and nominal Ø22/Ø19 mm pins.
5. **Proposed operating case.** 55 mm depth, 3 kW at 260 rpm and 90 kg assumed gross implement mass.
6. **Configuration.** Shaft, guard, bearings, depth roller and protected drive bodies are separate parts. Supplier drive selection and the operating rating require physical verification.
7. **Gearcase.** The revised end and side walls leave 4.5 mm and 5.5 mm respectively to the proposed Ø155 mm gear rim. Native readback preserves the shaft axes, tines, depth roller and mounting rails.
8. **Motor enclosure.** The separately removable motor shroud mounts on the case lid. Its collar has 2 mm nominal radial clearance and its top clears the motor envelope by 18 mm. It stays within Y ±102 mm and preserves the seeder rails.
9. **View.** [OpenCADStudio isometric](open-cad-studio-isometric.png).
