# 700 mm rear ground-driven seeder

**Representation.** Seven-outlet seeder with a formed hopper, interchangeable pocketed metering rings, a guarded chain layout and a floating ground roller.

1. **Native CAD.** [DWG 2018](j4-harrow-rear-ground-driven-seeder.dwg). SHA-256: fdd21600f719b3f3ac5a8c7e608d2deda4ae7b0419f05b84dcef91ef6bd5d114.
2. **Native checks.** 99 individually named part records passed the native geometry checks.
3. **Working arrangement.** Nominal 700 mm span. Seven replaceable Ø54 mm drum rings each use six nominal Ø6 mm blind seed pockets. The independent Ø130 mm trailing roller has a limited-angle mounting arm.
4. **Frame mount.** Headed Ø10 mm pins fit a proposed bore pair in each attachment rail. Axes are X = 524 and 554 mm and Z = 34 mm in the companion rotary harrow frame.
5. **Proposed operating case.** 17.22 L hopper geometry and 8 kg seed load. Metering estimates use assumed seed density, pocket fill and zero roller slip.
6. **Configuration.** Metering cassette, service cover and floating roller are separate parts. The two-implement combination needs an installed lift and stability check.
