# Category 1 light fork carrier

Detachable front fork carrier with two tines, carrier frame and pinned Category 1 mounting.

1. **CAD file.** [DWG 2018](j4-category1-light-fork-carrier.dwg). SHA-256 `2ab205ecfeaa91bedcad0969fe28bf7d5b0025ddec1da28da6ed60279e144efd`.
2. **Geometry and design case.** Native CAD contains 27 modeled solids. The installed front linkage uses Ø22 mm lower pins and spherical ends. The level-pickup assessment uses an assumed 85 kg gross fork load and a separate pair of rolling supports.
3. **Retention.** Two lower pins and the upper implement pin have headed bearings and removable keepers. Both original 49 mm lower cross-drill stations and all three pin axes remain unchanged. Native readback found clear withdrawal and wrench-approach envelopes at all three stations.
4. **View.** [OpenCADStudio isometric](open-cad-studio-isometric.png).

5. **Native display.** Revision 3 preserves the original solid geometry, attachment axes and placements. All 27 installed solids have functional layers. OpenCADStudio verified the effective RGB values, isolated each group and restored normal visibility.

   - `Display-Implement-Frame` — six crossbeam, upright and mast members, `#667584`.
   - `Display-Implement-Interface` — ten mounting lugs, ears and saddles, `#C6484D`.
   - `Display-Implement-Working-Surfaces` — two fork tines, `#4F80A6`.
   - `Display-Implement-Pins-and-Locks` — three headed pins and three removable keepers, `#B99DDD`.
   - `Display-Implement-Fasteners` — three locking nuts, `#A2A8AF`.
