## Geometry

J3f BMW chassis with 300 mm caster, detachable deck ledge and Fibox R2 cabinet on its rear crossmember. Four nominal M6 × 20 stacks secure the cabinet feet in the installed pose. The cabinet includes the Phoenix rail and a drawing-traced Pilz relay packaging reference.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Closed cabinet with the Pilz packaging reference.

The assembly contains 428 solid occurrences. The static check compares 20,832 sampled boundary-box pairs between the cabinet support and the retained chassis. Both crossmember ends meet the hitch side-boom bounds at Y = ±260 mm. The check covers the closed cabinet pose.
