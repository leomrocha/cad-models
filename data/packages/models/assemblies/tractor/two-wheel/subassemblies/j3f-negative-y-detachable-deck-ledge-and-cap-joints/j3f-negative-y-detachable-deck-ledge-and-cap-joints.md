# J3f negative-Y detachable deck ledge and two cap joints

[Native DWG 2018](j3f-negative-y-detachable-deck-ledge-and-cap-joints.dwg), SHA-256 `412f79109647f15a603478452b8b0332e12e17eb3f4f550b8a3cb3e70a0c8c5c`.

## Geometry

Detachable negative-Y recessed-captive J3f deck ledge with four receivers, two eccentric receiver caps, two posts and two M8 × 20 countersunk screws.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Nominal negative-Y detachable ledge and cap joints.

The two attachment axes are 536 mm apart. Each 90° seat is split between the 3 mm ledge and 3 mm cap, with a Ø12.25 mm match at their boundary. The nominal M8 screw head is recessed 0.91 mm and its tip projects 6.91 mm beyond the 8 mm receiver. The offset receiver clears the deck post by 0.5 mm in the longitudinal direction. This is a nominal fit proposal. Actual full-form engagement and joint strength require verification.
