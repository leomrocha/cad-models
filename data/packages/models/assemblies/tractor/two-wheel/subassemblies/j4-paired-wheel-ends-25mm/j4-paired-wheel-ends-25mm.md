# J4 paired wheel ends for 25 mm shafts

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-paired-wheel-ends-25mm`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion fit reference.

[Native DWG 2018](j4-paired-wheel-ends-25mm.dwg), SHA-256 `98e64af29ed105b12abf9a572dc2dc3d2bb17e35ce75ab1dce1ffb1379afc11c`.

## Geometry

Paired J4 25 mm wheel ends with inner retainer screws, washers and bearing spacers.

Millimetres. X along the chassis, Y positive left along the shaft axis, Z up.

There are 10 placed groups and 18 solid occurrences. The nominal M8 screws extend 13.91 mm past the inner shaft faces. The two heads have an axial separation of 7.82 mm. These values apply to the nominal sharp-cone model.

The bearing spans are placement references. The models represent the shaft and wheel flange hardware, including its bearing spacers.
