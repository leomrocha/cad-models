## Geometry

Paired J S03T splash panels with rails, clamps and fourteen square-ended EMKA 1010-12 edge-trim installation envelopes.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

Four trim definitions supply seven cuts per panel and 1930.4 mm of net profile. Each panel has its own selectable edge-trim layer. The section is a traced installation estimate.
