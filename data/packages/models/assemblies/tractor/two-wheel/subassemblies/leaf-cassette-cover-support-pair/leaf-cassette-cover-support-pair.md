# Leaf two-cassette terminal cover support frames

**Catalogue draft ID** `leaf-cassette-cover-support-pair`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](leaf-cassette-cover-support-pair.dwg), SHA-256 `d6aa724b950d20ebb11201969594ad4d4eb4e7ac0994d6015df4486e6c6f7d55`.

## Geometry

Paired Nissan Leaf cassette terminal cover support frames. Four uprights, four extension brackets, eight bridges and eight insulating blocks.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Millimetres in the cassette frame. X is U, Y is V, Z is N. Cassette centres are U = ±133 mm.

The inner brackets are 4 mm apart at the nominal cassette locations. Eight foot axes, eight bridge axes and eight insulating block axes match. The cover height is set by the installed terminal and cable envelope.
