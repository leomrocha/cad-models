## Geometry

J4 outboard splash panels and hardware with the detachable left fixing beam and its two M4 joints.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Full splash group with detachable left support proposal.

The native assembly contains 91 solids. The two left risers and left fixing beam replace three previous occurrences. The replacement group includes two nominal M4 screw envelopes.
