## Geometry

Raised BMW i3 94 Ah module reconstruction with its four shield uprights, the drilled tray floor and the 14-piece R3-relieved support group.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Installed module centre X = 75 mm.

Both tray rails meet the floor underside at Z = 575 mm. The floor top and four upright feet meet at Z = 578 mm. The 0.25 mm dielectric contact layer sits between the floor at Z = 578 mm and the reconstructed module base at Z = 578.25 mm. The saved native layer matches the twelve corners of the base outline and all four Ø8.5 mm bolt passages through the floor, layer and module base.

## Installed nominal shield

The four Ø4.5 mm shield-ear bores match the source upright axes at Z = 738.25 mm. The roof underside is 10 mm above the reconstructed OEM cover. The side opening follows the checked cable projection for the nominal 150 mm reference height. The separate dielectric layer remains installed.

## Upper shield fasteners

Four M4 × 16 screws, eight plain washers and four nylon-insert nuts form the upper shield joints. The nominal solid grip is 6 mm and the nominal modeled tip projection beyond each nut is 3.4 mm. The fastener assembly represents the nominal seating geometry.
