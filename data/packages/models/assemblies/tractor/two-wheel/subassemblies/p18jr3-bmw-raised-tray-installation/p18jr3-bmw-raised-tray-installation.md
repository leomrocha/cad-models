## Geometry

Raised BMW i3 94 Ah module reconstruction with its four shield uprights, the drilled tray floor and the 14-piece R3-relieved support group.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Installed module centre X = 75 mm.

Both tray rails meet the floor underside at Z = 575 mm. The floor top and four upright feet meet at Z = 578 mm. The reconstructed module base is at Z = 578.25 mm, 0.25 mm above the floor.
