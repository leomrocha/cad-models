## Geometry

Raised BMW i3 94 Ah module reconstruction with its four shield uprights, the drilled tray floor and the 14-piece R3-relieved support group.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Installed module centre X = 75 mm.

Both tray rails meet the floor underside at Z = 575 mm. The floor top and four upright feet meet at Z = 578 mm. The 0.25 mm dielectric contact layer sits between the floor at Z = 578 mm and the reconstructed module base at Z = 578.25 mm. The saved native layer matches the twelve corners of the base outline and all four Ø8.5 mm bolt passages through the floor, layer and module base.
