# J4 keyed Knott wheel flange with relieved washer for 25 mm shaft

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-keyed-wheel-flange-relieved-installation-25mm`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion assembly proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j4-keyed-wheel-flange-relieved-installation-25mm.dwg), SHA-256 `34bb8ab47edde5679d2844cf5b383e92b7ca68f3f47dd943e9d271157a7c88bc`.

## Geometry

J4 25 mm keyed shaft, Knott wheel flange, outer washer and nominal M10 × 25 retainer at the checked installed positions.

Millimetres. Shaft-local +Z runs from the inner shaft face toward the wheel.

The key projects 3 mm above the shaft into a 3.3 mm flange keyway. The end washer has 0.5 mm radial space in the pilot. The screw enters the shaft end by 20 mm, leaving 5 mm nominal tip space.
The washer bears on the flange. Its measured relief floor stands 0.5 mm off the nominal shaft end and has 2.7 mm radial reserve to the shaft.
