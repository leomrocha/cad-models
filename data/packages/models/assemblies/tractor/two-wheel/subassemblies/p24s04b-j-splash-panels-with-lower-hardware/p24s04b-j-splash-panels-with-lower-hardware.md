**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

Paired tractor splash panels, rails, clamps and fourteen edge trim cuts, with six lower joints. Each joint has an M6 × 16 screw, one outboard washer and a locknut.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The six lower fastener stacks share the saved panel and support hole axes. The nominal 6 mm shank has 0.25 mm radial clearance in the 6.5 mm bores. A 4.5 mm grip, 1.6 mm washer and 6 mm nut give 3.9 mm nominal protrusion with a 16 mm screw. The head bears on the sheet and the washer seats against the outboard support.
