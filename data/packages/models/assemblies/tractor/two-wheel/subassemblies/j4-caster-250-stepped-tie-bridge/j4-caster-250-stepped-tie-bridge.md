## Geometry

J4 250 mm caster bridge with a stepped upper tie, two 89 mm drop tubes, two bridge tubes and four root plates.

Tractor datum in millimetres. X points forward, Y left and Z up.

The two integral feet share the Z = 393 mm cheek bearing plane. The drop posts seat on the tie at Z = 411 mm and the bridge underside at Z = 500 mm. The nine installed solids use four catalogue definitions.
