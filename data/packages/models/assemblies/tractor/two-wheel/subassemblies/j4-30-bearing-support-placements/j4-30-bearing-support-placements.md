## Geometry

Four J4 30 mm SKF mounting references on four checked bearing support plates. The paired slots align with the support plate bores at 117.5 mm pitch. All four shaft datums lie at Z = 233 mm.

Millimetres. X along the chassis, Y along the wheel shafts, Z up.

The support plate bores meet the curved slot centre datums at four inverted stations. The bearing occurrences are catalogue mounting references.
