## Geometry

Two KLS7240N V1.21 projected-clearance references and their steel carrier assemblies on a drilled J3f frame core.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Nominal carrier hardware installed at the saved rail datums.

Four native M6 clamp screw axes align with the new Ø6.6 mm rail passages and compression sleeve bores. The sleeves seat at the two inner walls. The controller bodies retain the Figure 7 projected-clearance representation.
