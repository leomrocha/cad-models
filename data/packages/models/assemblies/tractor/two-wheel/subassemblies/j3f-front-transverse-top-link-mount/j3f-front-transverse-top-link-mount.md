**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j3f-front-transverse-top-link-mount.dwg), SHA-256 `2bdab8326100942aa97460328e1fa5a4d28575cf38498397e8772bbeafba49d5`.

## Geometry

J3f front attachment support with two fitted top-link cheeks. The cheeks share a transverse Ø19.5 mm bore axis.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Proposed top-link bracket with saved attachment support.

The two cheek roots seat flush on the retained carrier. Each face has 500 mm² of nominal contact. The transverse pin centre is [420, 0, 1010] mm and the gap between cheek faces is 52 mm. The gap is a packaging assumption for the selected linkage eye. The model shows the mating steel geometry.
