## Geometry

J3f recessed deck and outboard foot hardware with 300 mm caster. Exact-position replacement at the four right front foot axes.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Nominal installed pose. Captive joint strength evaluated separately.

Native verification counts 226 retained solid occurrences in 13 root groups. The checked source placements outside the replaced group are preserved.
Four outboard M10 × 35 screws enter two internal nut bars. The captive joints and lifting load path require structural checks.
The four screw tips have 15 mm nominal Y separation from the right motor backbone. The 11,658 sampled deck-to-chassis solid-bound comparisons yielded zero positive overlaps.
