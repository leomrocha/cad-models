## Geometry

J4 recessed deck and lifting supports with front hardware for the rear top-mount chassis.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Rear uprights at their original chassis positions.

The 86 solids include two recessed panels, deck supports, lifting stanchions and 30 front fastener pieces. The panels span 600 × 400 mm at Z = 773 mm.
The saved rear upright positions seat on the four top-mounted feet supplied by the chassis. Native checks preserved every retained occurrence and its placement.
