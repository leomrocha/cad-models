# J3f Motenergy chassis with rearward 250 mm caster

**Catalogue draft ID** `j3f-motenergy-chassis-with-relocated-250mm-caster`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion integration proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j3f-motenergy-chassis-with-relocated-250mm-caster.dwg), SHA-256 `6cf1ce7997d22cec500e3595e3e7f4ce37c1df55884a50d7e4e35e4bca9b1e1d`.

## Geometry

J3f raised frame with both Motenergy ME0907 motor references, attachment mounts, H390 hitch tubes and relocated 250 mm caster.

Millimetres. X points forward, Y left and Z up. Zero caster tilt and nominal motor adjustment.

The 92 installed solids include four relocated caster posts seated on the rails. The assembled caster and left motor nominal X envelopes are 4.5 mm apart. In each motor stack the Ø114.3 mm pilot has 0.35 mm nominal radial space in the Ø115 mm plate aperture. The shaft passes through the moving plate and the fixed backbone opening.
