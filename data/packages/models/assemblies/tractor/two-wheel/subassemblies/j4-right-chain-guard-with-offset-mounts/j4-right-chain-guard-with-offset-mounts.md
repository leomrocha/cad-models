# J4 right chain guard with offset upper mounts

Revision 1. Discussion installation reference. Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

3. **Offset mounting.** The four tab bores and notch floors align with the proposed socket positions. The nominal Ø14 mm socket has 0.5 mm side clearance between the braces and 0.25 mm bottom clearance at the rim.
4. **Scope.** The moving guard body and lid are represented. The lower rim is 158.762396 mm below the axle-height boundary. Check complete withdrawal against the fixed mounts and drivetrain.
