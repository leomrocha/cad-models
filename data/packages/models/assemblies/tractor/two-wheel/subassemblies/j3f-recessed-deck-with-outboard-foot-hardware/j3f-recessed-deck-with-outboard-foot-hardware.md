## Geometry

J3f recessed deck with outboard foot hardware. Exact-position replacement at the four right front foot axes.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Nominal installed pose. Captive joint strength evaluated separately.

Native verification counts 139 retained solid occurrences in 17 root groups. The checked source placements outside the replaced group are preserved.
Four outboard M10 × 35 screws enter two internal nut bars. The captive joints and lifting load path require structural checks.
