## Geometry

J3f Kelly controller carriers with four outboard clamps, checked alongside the retained Motenergy motor plates.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

Four outboard M6 × 45 screw envelopes and four rail inserts replace the inboard clamp assemblies. The four original outer washers remain. All new insert seats and screw axes share the retained hole stations. This proposal covers nominal geometry and the saved motor plate positions.
