## Geometry

Structural S tow support tube frame. Two 230 mm side booms, one 520 mm rear crossbar and one 600 mm rear frame outrigger.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Tow support tube frame in the Structural S chassis frame.

The side booms touch the outrigger at X = −340 mm. The crossbar meets each boom at Y = ±260 mm. The installation uses the nominal tube cuts. The tow ball, terminal plate and caster sweep belong to the complete hitch installation.
