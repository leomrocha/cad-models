## Geometry

Right J4 splash sheet, lowered 340 mm centre crossbar and a 16 mm M6 compression sleeve. The sheet and tube share a Ø6.5 mm fixing axis through X = 0 mm and Z = 694 mm.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Right centre fixing placement proposal.

The crossbar occupies Z = 684–704 mm. Its sheet contact face is at Y = −175 mm. The sleeve spans Y = −193 to −177 mm between the tube walls, with zero nominal end gap. The native readback confirmed three closed bodies and the common fixing axis.
