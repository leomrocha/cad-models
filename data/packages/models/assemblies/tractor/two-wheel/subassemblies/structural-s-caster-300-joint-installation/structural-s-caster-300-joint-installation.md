# Structural S 300 mm caster joint installation

**Catalogue draft ID** `structural-s-caster-300-joint-installation`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion installation proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](structural-s-caster-300-joint-installation.dwg), SHA-256 `db713d93c12baca8945af53a69f58e91f6ae0d167edbf79dfc32df0f76aa054f`.

## Geometry

300 mm Structural S caster joint with pin keepers, wheel mounting reference, clevis, webs, frame doubler and rail sleeves.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Fitted caster arm in the ground-working zero setting.

The 24 solid occurrences include a wheel envelope. The 12 reference primitives identify the supplier mounting datums.
The arm is at zero setting. Each cheek preserves the three −20°, 0° and +20° setting axes. The brace has 0.5 mm nominal lateral clearance to each cheek face.
The two keeper axes pass through the matching pin cross-bores. Each keeper fits within the nominal web access radius.
The four frame sleeves are aligned to the mounting stations. The chassis fastener stack, supplier fork and swivel internals use separate models.
