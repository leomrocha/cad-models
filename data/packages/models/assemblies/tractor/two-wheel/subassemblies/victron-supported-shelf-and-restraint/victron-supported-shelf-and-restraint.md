## Geometry

Padded Victron battery shelf and bolted upper restraint on two transverse angles and four post base plates.

Shelf-local millimetres. X joins the two battery terminals, Y opposes the terminal normal, and Z points up from the shelf top.

Two nominal 30 × 30 × 3 mm angles seat under the shelf at Z = −3 mm. Their nominal shelf contact widths are 30 mm and 19.15 mm. The four 3 mm base plates support the posts at Z = 0 mm.
