## Geometry

Paired J S03T splash installation on two machined raised-frame rails. Six nominal clamp joints each contain an M6 backing block, washer and M6 × 16 screw.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

All six nominal clamp axes and bearing planes align. The specified full-form dimensions give 5 mm overlap at each clamp. The fastener models represent the nominal thread envelope.
