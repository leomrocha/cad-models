## Geometry

J4 left splash fixing beam, two handed riser placements and two nominal M4 × 25 screws.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Nominal detachable beam support assembly.

The two screws sit at X = −211 and 211 mm, Y = 315.5 mm. The measured under-head bearing plane is Z = 580 mm. The nominal screw envelopes penetrate 10 mm into the risers. The riser bores represent tap pilots.
