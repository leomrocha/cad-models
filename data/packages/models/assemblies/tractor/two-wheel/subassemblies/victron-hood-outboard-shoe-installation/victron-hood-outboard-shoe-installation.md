## Geometry

Victron reference hoods with paired outboard brackets and their separate angle shoes. Four checked screw axes join the 3 mm bracket feet and 3 mm shoe flanges.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Bank-frame millimetres. X is U, Y is N and Z is V. Floor top is V = 0 mm.

The two shoe flanges cover the full extent of the bracket feet. The closest shoe edge is 26.097 mm from the projected battery case end. The shoes provide mounting faces for separate frame connections.
