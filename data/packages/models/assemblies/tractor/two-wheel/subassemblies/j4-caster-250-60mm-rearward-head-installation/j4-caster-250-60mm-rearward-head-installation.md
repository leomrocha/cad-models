## Geometry

J4 250 mm caster, mounted at zero tilt with both pins.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Ground-working zero arm setting.

The installed support keeps the 680 mm² nominal tie contact per side. The head sits 60 mm rearward and seats over 87.5 mm of arm length. The saved CAD shows 0° tilt.

The stepped bridge and its two drop posts retain the upper cheek bearing planes. At zero tilt the hitch lateral tubes touch the outboard root-plate boundaries at Y = ±176 mm.
