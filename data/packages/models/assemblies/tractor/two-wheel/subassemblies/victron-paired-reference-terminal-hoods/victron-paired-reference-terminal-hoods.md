## Geometry

Two Victron terminal hoods on four insulating posts at 220.2 mm source STEP terminal pitch. All four support seats and bore axes share the nominal n = 30 mm plane.

Millimetres. X joins the terminal axes, Y spans the terminal plane and Z points outward. X = 0 mm is the terminal midpoint.

The cover bodies have a 174.2 mm gap and the nearest mounting ears have a 134.2 mm gap. The cover references use the assumed 20 × 20 × 20 mm lug envelope.
