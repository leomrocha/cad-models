## Geometry

J4 25 mm branch rear bearing hanger preview assembly. Four lower cantilevers, four rear posts and four upper links placed on the checked bearing support group. The 46 mm left outer cantilever supports the shifted rear post and link while retaining the bearing station.

Millimetres. X along the chassis, Y along the wheel shafts, Z up.

The nominal cantilever, post and upper link contact planes align in all four stations. The SKF geometry represents mounting datums.
