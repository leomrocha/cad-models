# J4 Kelly controller carriers on guarded rails

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-kelly-carrier-and-guard-rail-installation`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j4-kelly-carrier-and-guard-rail-installation.dwg), SHA-256 `626b06fff3fa01af2cf3e9261ba94526800cd2449eae643cada535efcbbdff4f`.

## Geometry

Two Kelly KLS7240N V1.21 controller envelopes and their carriers on the guarded J4 rails with four outboard clamps.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Nominal controller carriers, guard mounts and rail openings.

Four Ø6.6 mm rail openings align with the outboard M6 screw axes. The left forward insert has a 1 mm nominal X gap to the enclosing guard-sleeve bound. The check screened 5,440 controller and carrier versus guard and foot solid pairs using sampled boundary boxes and found zero positive volume box overlaps.
