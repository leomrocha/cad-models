# J3f Tyro Gemini 1S raised and outboard cradle proposal

Revision 1. Discussion placement proposal. Dimensions are in millimetres. X points forward, Y points left and Z points up.

1. **Proposed placement.** The enclosure envelope, carrier, seat, retainers, pads, side stops and retained fixture hardware translate 30 mm outward and 75 mm upward. The case spans X = −325 to −165, Y = 240 to 300 and Z = 555 to 705. [Tyro Gemini 1S technical specification](https://www.tyroremotes.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Gemini-EN-1.1.pdf)
2. **Model scope.** The 61 retained solids reuse the checked receiver cradle. The original two bridge parts and their eight E05 fastener occurrences were removed from this placement proposal. The new attachment geometry and actual connector access use separate design inputs.
3. **Checks.** The measured translated cradle bounds agree with the checked nominal offset. The sampled chassis screen gives 6 mm minimum separating-axis margin on both caster layouts. The 80 mm outward case-only service screen covers the chassis obstacle envelopes after release of the fixture retainers.
