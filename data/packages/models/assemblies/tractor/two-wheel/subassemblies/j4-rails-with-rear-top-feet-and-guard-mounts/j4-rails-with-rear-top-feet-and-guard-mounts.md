## Geometry

Both J4 rails with four rear top-mounted outrigger feet and eight offset guard joints.

Millimetres. X along chassis rails, Y positive left and Z up.

The nominal C129 M6 × 55 screw projects 2.8 mm beyond the locknut. The 34 mm sleeve spans the rail cavity.

Each rail screw axis is 22 mm forward of the guard mounting axis. The stepped foot and offset support leg share a 60 mm² nominal seat.

The 82 solid occurrences include two rails and eight offset stacks. The native checks cover 16 rail wall holes. Each foot has 887.617 mm² of nominal flat rail contact.

Native readback also checked 24 deck foot wall openings across the two rails. The right-front rail screw is at X = 215 mm and Y = −150 mm. The installed sleeve envelope clears each adjacent deck nut bar by 4 mm in X.

Four rear outrigger feet share eight vertical M10 screw axes. Native measurements confirm the plate and rail openings, washer seats, blind bore ends and screw tips. The 33 mm nut bars leave 1 mm nominal insertion space, and screw tips stop 3 mm before the blind ends. The bar retention detail and load qualification are separate work.
