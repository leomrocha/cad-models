## Geometry

J4 splash-panel screw, Ø12 × 5.5 mm spacer and ISO 7089 M6 washer.

Millimetres. Local Z points outward from the sheet face at Z = 0.

The nominal screw enters the receiving seat by 7.4 mm and sits 0.6 mm short of the 8 mm back plane. Measured screw length and spacer thickness limits are part of the installation proposal.
