## Geometry

Fourteen-solid front support with lower pin cheeks, mast and transverse upper carrier.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Front mount in common raised chassis frame.

The native checks place all sleeves concentrically on their fixing axes. Both sleeve ends coincide with the nominal inner faces of their crossbar.
