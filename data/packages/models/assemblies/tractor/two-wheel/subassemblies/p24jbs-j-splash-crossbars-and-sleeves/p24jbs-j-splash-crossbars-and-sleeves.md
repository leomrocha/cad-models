**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

Paired J splash-panel upper supports for the lifting-post arrangement. Four 50 mm outer crossbars, two 340 mm centre crossbars and six 16 mm compression sleeves.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

The native checks place all sleeves concentrically on their fixing axes. Both sleeve ends coincide with the nominal inner faces of their crossbar.
