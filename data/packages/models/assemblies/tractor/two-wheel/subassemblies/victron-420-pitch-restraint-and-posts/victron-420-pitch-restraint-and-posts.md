# Victron 420 mm restraint bridges and four capped posts

**Catalogue draft ID** `victron-420-pitch-restraint-and-posts`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](victron-420-pitch-restraint-and-posts.dwg), SHA-256 `49ad145db48c161b9390a8fc8ec367b3b76a75bfbdeb73693daced3f5207f2b0`.

## Geometry

Two removable Victron restraint bridges on four capped angle posts. Three upper pads seat on the main-case contact planes.

Shelf-local millimetres. X joins the two battery terminals, Y opposes the terminal normal, and Z points up from the shelf top.

The bridge and cap bores align on four axes at X = ±210 mm. Each joint has 6 mm of nominal solid grip. The terminal-side post legs clear the projected hood-mount envelope by 8.3 mm and 5.398 mm.
