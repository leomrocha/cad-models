## Geometry

Two supported Victron battery shelves and their upper restraints, with a proposed 5 mm vertical gap between the support assemblies.

Shelf-local millimetres. X joins the two battery terminals, Y opposes the terminal normal, and Z points up from the shelf top.

The shelf top faces are 232.216 mm apart, measured along Z. The two support assemblies span a combined 459.432 mm vertically.
