## Geometry

J4 raised frame, Motenergy mounts and 25 mm wheel ends in a checked interference study.

Millimetres. X along the rails, Y positive left, Z up.

The motor axes are at X = −30 mm and X = 180 mm, both at Z = 430 mm.
Both backbone seating planes align with the rail inner faces at Y = ±130 mm. Four material overlaps are present in this nominal pose. Both outer cantilevers cross their fixed backbones. The left outer post and upper link cross the left moving plate.
