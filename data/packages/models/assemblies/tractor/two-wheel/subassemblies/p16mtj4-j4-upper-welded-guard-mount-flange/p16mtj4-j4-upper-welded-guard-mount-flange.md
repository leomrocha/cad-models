# J4 upper welded chain-guard mount flange

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

J4 upper chain-guard flange with a vertical sheet, rail foot and 1 mm-leg fillet envelope.

Millimetres. X along the frame, Y positive left, Z up.

Both fixing bores are Ø6.5 mm. The guard bore is normal to Y at Z = 489.036290 mm. The rail bore is normal to Z at Y = 150 mm. The rail contact plane is Z = 500 mm.
The nominal gap from the top of the Ø12 mm washer envelope to the weld is 0.963710 mm.
