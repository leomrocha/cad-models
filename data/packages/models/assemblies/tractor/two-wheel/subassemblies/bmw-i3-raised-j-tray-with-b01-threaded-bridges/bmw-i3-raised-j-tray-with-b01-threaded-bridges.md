# BMW i3 raised J tray with upper threaded bridges and shield

**Catalogue draft ID** `bmw-i3-raised-j-tray-with-b01-threaded-bridges`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](bmw-i3-raised-j-tray-with-b01-threaded-bridges.dwg), SHA-256 `77d5fe79cf7599605e23d8668bf67ef6263da5f1a402b053eff8ad0ac8156246`.

## Geometry

BMW i3 94 Ah battery on the revised raised J tray with two threaded tab bridges, four inverted battery-tab screws and a terminal shield on four bolted uprights.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Nominal battery and full shield stack on the revised raised tray support.

The four inverted M8 × 30 screws use a nominal 13.25 mm solid grip and project 7.15 mm beyond the bridge tops. Each 11 mm bridge is located by two axes. The bridge edges clear the reconstructed end plates by 0.75 mm in the nominal model. The installation uses the retained insulated contact layer and upper shield geometry. Bridge material, load capacity and actual donor tab geometry require verification for fabrication.
