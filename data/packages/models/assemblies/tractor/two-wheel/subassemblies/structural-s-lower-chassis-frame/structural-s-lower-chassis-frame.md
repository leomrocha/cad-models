## Geometry

Structural S lower chassis with two 600 mm rails and three 260 mm crossmembers. The five nominal 40 × 40 × 3 mm hollow sections form a 600 × 340 mm frame.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Lower frame in the Structural S chassis datum.

The rails are 260 mm apart at their inner faces. The crossmember centres are X = −280, 0 and +280 mm. The frame spans Z = 233 to 273 mm and the footprint diagonal is 689.638 mm nominal. Six end joints share their contact planes. Weld dimensions depend on the joint loads.
