## Geometry

J3f front chassis-side attachment support. The root-relieved braces and proposed upper pivot carrier fit the lower chassis.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Chassis-side support in the common raised J3f datum.

The doubler sits on X = 325 mm. The two Ø22.5 mm lower chassis pivot bores share X = 393 mm and Z = 590 mm. The proposed Ø19.5 mm upper chassis pivot crosses Y = 0 mm at Z = 1010 mm. The upper carrier contacts 1,680 mm² of the mast flat. Linkage motion requires the mating links.
