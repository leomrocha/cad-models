# J4 25 mm paired wheel ends and fastened hanger fit

## Geometry

Paired 25 mm J4 wheel ends fitted to the fastened bearing hangers.

Millimetres. X along the chassis, Y positive left along the shafts, Z up.

The four bearing axis datums coincide with the shafts at Z = 233 mm.
The 864 rotating-to-stationary sampled boundary-box pairs have a minimum separation of 16.5 mm at the saved pose.
SKF bearing units are represented by source dimension datums.
