# J4 25 mm shifted left hanger assembly with J03 fasteners

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-25-bearing-hangers-with-shifted-left-post-j03-stack`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j4-25-bearing-hangers-with-shifted-left-post-j03-stack.dwg), SHA-256 `23ef574a17db3ac8b646bce9feb2fb418a536ebd932b506803fe5c2339e60be1`.

## Geometry

J4 25 mm bearing hangers with eight nominal J03 screw stacks, each using an M10 × 65 screw, two plain washers and one locknut. The left outer hanger post and upper link are shifted inward by 11 mm, with the same eight fastener axes.

Millimetres. X along chassis, Y along the shaft, Z up.

Native placement checks confirm the eight nominal screw stacks. Screw tips lie at Z = 237.5 mm and extend 4 mm past the nuts. The SKF geometry supplies mounting datums.
