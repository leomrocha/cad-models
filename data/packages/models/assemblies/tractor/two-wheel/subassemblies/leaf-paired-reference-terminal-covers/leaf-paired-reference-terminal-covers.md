## Geometry

Two Leaf reference terminal covers seated on eight insulating blocks. The assembly preserves the specified case spacing and cover-support frames.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Cassette-frame millimetres. X is U, Y is V and Z is N. Cover centres are U = ±133 mm.

All eight cover mounting axes align with their insulating blocks at n = 172 mm. The conservative shell zone check covers 432 support-part pairs. Harness crossings and live terminal clearance use the installed terminal geometry.
