# J4 A07 upper guard flange and rail fastener stack

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-a07-guard-flange-rail-fastener-stack`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion installation proposal.

## Geometry

One A07 upper chain-guard flange with matching sleeve, screw, washers and locknut.

Millimetres. X along chassis rails, Y positive left and Z up.

The nominal C129 M6 × 55 screw projects 2.8 mm beyond the locknut. The 34 mm sleeve spans the rail cavity.
