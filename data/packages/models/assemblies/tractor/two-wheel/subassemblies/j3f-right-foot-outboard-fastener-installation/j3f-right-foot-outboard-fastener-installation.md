# J3f right foot outboard fastener installation

**Catalogue draft ID** `j3f-right-foot-outboard-fastener-installation`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j3f-right-foot-outboard-fastener-installation.dwg), SHA-256 `90f270098f131dd8748de5db49db8ab0b048c81abb14073828178ed6ac136ed2`.

## Geometry

Two internal nut bars with four outboard M10 × 35 screws and four M10 washers.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Installed fastener subset shared by the 250 mm and 300 mm caster variants.

The four screw tips stop 3 mm before the blind bottoms, with 15 mm nominal separation along Y from the right motor backbone. The nut bars fit inside the rail envelope. Weld and joint strength require separate checks.
