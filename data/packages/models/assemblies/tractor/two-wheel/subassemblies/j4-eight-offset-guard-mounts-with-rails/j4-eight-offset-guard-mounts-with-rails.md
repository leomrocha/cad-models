# J4 eight offset upper guard joints on raised rails

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-eight-offset-guard-mounts-with-rails`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion installation proposal.

## Geometry

Two J4 raised chassis rails with eight checked offset guard fastener stacks.

Millimetres. X along chassis rails, Y positive left and Z up.

The nominal C129 M6 × 55 screw projects 2.8 mm beyond the locknut. The 34 mm sleeve spans the rail cavity.

Each rail screw axis is 22 mm forward of the guard mounting axis. The stepped foot and offset support leg share a 60 mm² nominal seat.

The 58 solid occurrences include two rails and eight offset stacks. The native checks cover 16 rail wall holes. Each foot has 887.617 mm² of nominal flat rail contact.
