**Design status** Discussion installation proposal.

## Geometry

J4 300 mm caster bridge with a stepped upper tie, two 39 mm drop tubes, two bridge tubes and four root plates.

Tractor datum in millimetres. X points forward, Y left and Z up.

The two integral feet share the Z = 443 mm cheek bearing plane. The drop posts seat on the tie at Z = 461 mm and the bridge underside at Z = 500 mm. The nine installed solids use four catalogue definitions.
