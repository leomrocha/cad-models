## Geometry

Fibox R2 cabinet support with four nominal M6 × 20 cabinet-foot screws, eight plain washers and four DIN 985 M6 nuts.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Installed-pose proposal.

Four M6 × 20 screw stacks share the measured foot and carrier axes at Y = ±130 mm and Z = 480 or 740 mm. Each nominal screw tip projects 2.8 mm beyond the nut. This model records the installed pose. Installation travel and tool access require separate geometric checks.
