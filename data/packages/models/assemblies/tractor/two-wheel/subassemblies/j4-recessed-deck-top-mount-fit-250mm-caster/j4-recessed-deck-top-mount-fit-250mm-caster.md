## Geometry

J4 deck and lifting supports on the 250 mm caster chassis with rear top-mounted feet.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Recessed deck in the nominal installed position.

All four rear uprights seat at Z = 548 mm. The two panels span 600 × 400 mm at Z = 773 mm.
The 404 solids include two chain-space envelopes. The installed-pose screen compared 27,348 sampled boundary boxes. Nominal screw shanks, sleeves and nut bars account for all 14 overlapping front rail bounds. The eight modeled screw shanks have 0.5 mm of nominal radial clearance in the rail bores.
