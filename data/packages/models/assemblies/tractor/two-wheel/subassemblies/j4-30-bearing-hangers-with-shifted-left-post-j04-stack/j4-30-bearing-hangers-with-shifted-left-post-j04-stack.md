# J4 30 mm shifted left hanger assembly with J04 fasteners

## Geometry

J4 30 mm bearing hangers with eight nominal J04 stacks of M12 × 70 countersunk screws, plain washers and locknuts. The left outer hanger post and upper link are shifted inward by 11 mm, with the same eight fastener axes.

Millimetres. X along chassis, Y along the shaft, Z up.

The equal-cone model places each head top 1.035 mm below the seat mouth and each tip at Z = 235.865 mm. The SKF geometry supplies mounting datums. Purchased head seating and tool access require an installed check.
