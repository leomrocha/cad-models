# Fibox R2 cabinet and fastened crossmember support

**Catalogue draft ID** `fibox-r2-cabinet-and-crossmember-support`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](fibox-r2-cabinet-and-crossmember-support.dwg), SHA-256 `d4b3a9f318ac79c33c2f6d32581de8ba3e455d2804a1c1d1f055d5a415264643`.

## Geometry

Fibox ARCA 203015T cabinet, rear carrier plate, drilled crossmember, two internal receiver blocks and four M6 × 16 carrier screw stacks.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Installed R2 cabinet mounting proposal.

All four retained Fibox feet meet the carrier at X = −460 mm. Four nominal M6 × 16 screws pass through the carrier and crossmember wall and enter the two receivers by 8.4 mm. The cabinet foot clamps, receiver retention and door swing are separate checks.
