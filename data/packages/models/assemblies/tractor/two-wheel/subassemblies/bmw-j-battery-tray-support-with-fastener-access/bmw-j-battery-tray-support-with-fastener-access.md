# BMW J battery tray support with battery-tab and shield-upright access

**Catalogue draft ID** `bmw-j-battery-tray-support-with-fastener-access`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](bmw-j-battery-tray-support-with-fastener-access.dwg), SHA-256 `41f56616b48577c31a412e40ea957aefd58ffb92aadc71b6669cb8198af014af`.

## Geometry

BMW J tray floor, two drilled support angles, four riser pads and eight relocated braces. The support has four battery-tab axes and four removable shield-upright axes.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Nominal tray-support proposal with two relocated braces at each pad.

The two brace stations at each pad are moved inward to clear the proposed underside tool approaches. A Ø21 mm battery socket guard has 1.25 mm minimum radial clearance to the brace envelopes. A Ø10 mm shield tool guard has 0.288 mm nominal clearance to the adjacent battery washer envelope. Each guard spans Z = 547–572 mm. The larger battery socket approach assumes the shield-upright hardware has been removed. The group is a nominal support proposal with relocated braces.
