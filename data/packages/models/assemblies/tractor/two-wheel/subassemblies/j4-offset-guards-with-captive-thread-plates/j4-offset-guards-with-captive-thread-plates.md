# J4 offset chain guards with captive threaded mounting plates

Revision 1. Discussion installation proposal. Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

1. **Assembly.** Both offset chain guards, both raised rails, eight unpocketed rail stacks, eight captive threaded plates and sixteen removable A06 fastener pieces.
2. **Geometry.** Five coaxial checks passed at each station. Each captive plate has a 6 mm long nominal thread envelope and 0.25 mm clearance below the rail foot.
3. **Access.** Each guard screw uses the outside socket path. The narrowest nominal reserve in the checked socket envelope is 0.25 mm at the rim notch. Weld, thread engagement and screw locking require separate qualification.
4. **DWG SHA-256.** `67daf5b02799faa416bee2e3202fabec8210525652766698c2f43e75d00f8815`.
