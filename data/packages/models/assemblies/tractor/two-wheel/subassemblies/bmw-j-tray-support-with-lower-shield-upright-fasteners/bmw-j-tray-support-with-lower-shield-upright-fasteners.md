# BMW J tray support with four lower shield upright joints

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

BMW tray support with four shield uprights, four M4 × 25 screws, eight plain washers and four DIN 985 M4 locknut envelopes.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Four nominal C02 joints on the revised support.

The four lower joints use a 9 mm nominal solid stack through the upright foot, tray floor and support angle. Each screw tip projects 9.4 mm below its nut. The known source upright transforms and all four hardware stacks were checked in the saved CAD.
