**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

Two removable Victron restraint bridges and their three upper pads placed at the verified planar main-case bearing faces.

Shelf-local millimetres. X joins the two battery terminals, Y opposes the terminal normal, and Z points up from the shelf top.

Two bolt axes per bridge sit 390 mm apart. Three pads bear at Z = 162.216 mm and the bridge top reaches Z = 194.216 mm. The separate frame-side mounts establish the specified joint grip and preload.
