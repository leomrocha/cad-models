## Geometry

J4 right wheel-end stack for a 30 mm shaft, with a keyed sprocket carrier hub blank and retained spacer.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. The handed shaft runs outboard from Y = ±10 mm.

The hub and key occupy outward stations 132.2–172.2 mm. The outboard spacer reaches the wheel hub at 229 mm.
The nominal axial seating chain and the 0.3 mm gap above the sprocket hub key were checked at the saved pose.
