# J4 offset guard body withdrawal reference

Revision 1. Conditional service reference. Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

1. **Paths.** Each side has a 0.5 mm outward and 1 mm downward release, followed by 154.8 mm of straight outward travel. Native lines mark the reference paths.
2. **Envelopes.** Left and right swept enclosing boxes and path stages occupy six named layers. The embedded seated model supplies the installation datum.
3. **Release condition.** Remove the wheel, flange, retainers, lid, guard-side fasteners, chain and both sprockets from the moving side. Clearances describe the nominal bare-shaft geometry. Sprocket release and wrench access require separate checks.
