# J4 offset chain guards fitted to pocketed rail mounts

Revision 1. Discussion installation reference. Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

1. **Geometry.** Both chain guards are fitted to the two raised rails with eight A06 fastener stacks. Native checks located the guard-tab and support-leg bores on the screw axis at every station. Each inboard washer clears its pocket ceiling by 0.75 mm in the nominal assembly.
2. **Scope.** The recorded checks cover seated geometry. Nut access, chassis withdrawal and joint loading have separate checks.
3. **DWG SHA-256.** `7c3a345bda109eb84015a65559bd33cd7141f76d16ad14bcbcf07794592aefc2`.
