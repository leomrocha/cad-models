## Geometry

J3f rear attachment support with Category 1 link-centre layout. Named datum layers locate the proposed transverse top-link pin and show the checked poses.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left, Z up. Link lines reference shoulder stations.

The checked layout has seventeen support solids and 24 datum lines. The proposed Y-axis top-link pin centre is [-400, 0, 1010] mm. Link lines use a 570 mm assumed distance between lower pin centres. They represent calculated pose geometry. The upper pin axis is a design datum for the mating bracket.
