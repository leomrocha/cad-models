**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

J3f rear attachment support with two fitted top-link cheeks. The cheeks share a transverse Ø19.5 mm bore axis.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Proposed top-link bracket with saved attachment support.

The two cheek roots seat flush on the retained carrier. Each face has 500 mm² of nominal contact. The transverse pin centre is [-400, 0, 1010] mm and the gap between cheek faces is 52 mm. The gap is a packaging assumption for the selected linkage eye. The model shows the mating steel geometry.
