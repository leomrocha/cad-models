## Geometry

J4 raised frame, relieved Motenergy motor module and 25 mm wheel ends with shifted left hanger.

Millimetres. X along the rails, Y positive left, Z up.

The motor axes are at X = −30 mm and X = 180 mm, both at Z = 430 mm.
Both backbone seating planes align with the rail inner faces at Y = ±130 mm.
The nominal vertical gap between each cantilever and its backbone is 7.5 mm. The post and upper link retain 1 mm lateral clearance to the moving plate.
The represented sampled boundary boxes have no positive overlap at the saved pose or through the −15 mm to +15 mm Z travel screen.
