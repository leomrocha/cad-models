# J4 30 mm drive and 250 mm caster chassis with rear top-mounted feet

Revision 1. Discussion installation proposal for the J4 30 mm drive shafts and 250 mm caster. Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up.

1. **Installed pose.** The estimated 58-pitch left and 66-pitch right chain loops set the motor axes at Z = 426.92 mm and Z = 429.65 mm. Both motor and moving-plate pairs use these positions. The source has a zero-tilt caster.
2. **Checked geometry.** The assembly contains 316 chassis solids and two chain clearance solids. The saved guard installation replaces the original upper rail mounts in the raised chassis. Both nominal chain spaces are placed inside the guard cavities. The outer guard extends 158.762396 mm below the axle-height boundary. The installed rails carry the deck foot openings and the right-front guard mount is shifted by 17 mm. Four top-mounted rear outrigger feet and their screws and nut bars are installed.
3. **Clearance screen.** Each chain-space solid was screened against all 316 installed body boundary sample boxes. The nearest sampled bounds give at least 1.36 mm. The checks represent nominal packaging with sampled boundaries. Fitting a physical chain requires the exact sprocket interface and a slack check.
