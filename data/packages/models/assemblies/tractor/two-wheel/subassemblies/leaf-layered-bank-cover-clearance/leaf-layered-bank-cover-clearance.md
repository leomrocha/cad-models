## Geometry

Two layered Nissan Leaf cassettes with nominal clamp hardware, the common floor and paired terminal covers. Terminal contact faces and cover-shell clearance are checked in the bank frame.

Bank-frame millimetres. X is U, Y is N and Z is V. Floor top is V = 0 mm.

The 24 power contacts and 12 centre taps lie at N = 162 mm, 10 mm from the inner cover roof. The nominal restrained stack is 211.75 mm high. This clearance uses bare contacts, with the conductors and lugs absent from the calculation.
