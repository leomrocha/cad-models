## Geometry

Victron battery restraint with four M8 × 30 screws, eight ISO 7089 M8 washers and four DIN 985 M8 locknut nominal references at the bridge-to-cap joints.

Shelf-local millimetres. X joins the battery terminals, Y opposes the terminal normal, Z points up.

The four nominal joints have 6 mm solid grip and 12.8 mm screw protrusion below the nut. The Ø8 mm nominal shank has 0.25 mm radial clearance in the bridge and cap bores and 0.20 mm in the washers.
