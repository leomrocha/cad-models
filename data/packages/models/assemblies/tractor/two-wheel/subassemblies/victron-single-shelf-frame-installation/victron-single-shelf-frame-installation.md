# Victron single supported shelf fastened to the Structural S chassis

**Catalogue draft ID** `victron-single-shelf-frame-installation`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](victron-single-shelf-frame-installation.dwg), SHA-256 `c90ca6520efdcf0ca715dcfa10f9f612eeb00a11271995371fdabe83b238e39b`.

## Geometry

Padded Victron battery shelf and upper restraint mounted to the Structural S lower frame with two drilled support angles and four M8 flange joints.

Shelf-local millimetres. X joins the two battery terminals, Y opposes the terminal normal, and Z points up from the shelf top.

The four nominal frame joints place M8 × 30 screws through matching Ø8.5 mm bores. Each joint has 9 mm of solid grip and 9.8 mm of screw projection beyond the locknut. The upper restraint reaches Z = 520.216 mm in the saved frame coordinates.
