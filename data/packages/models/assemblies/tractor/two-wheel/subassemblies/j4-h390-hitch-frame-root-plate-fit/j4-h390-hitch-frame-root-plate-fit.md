## Geometry

J H390 tow support tube frame. Two side booms, two lateral outriggers, two lowering posts and a 600 mm rear crossbar.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Tow support tube frame in the J H390 chassis frame.

The crossbar top is Z = 430 mm and the side-boom undersides are Z = 500 mm. The two lowering posts connect those planes. The plan cover opening envelope with 2 mm padding gives an 8.813 mm nominal vertical gap above the crossbar at this layout.
