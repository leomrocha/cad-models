## Geometry

LS-PS310K source-bound external caster fit assembly with nine installed solids.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Origin on the ground plane below the swivel axis.

The fitted exterior includes the top mounting plate, swivel body, two fork legs, wheel, axle, two hub spacers and retaining nut.
The nominal front-to-back caster envelope sweeps a radius of at most 240.260 mm about the swivel datum.
The radius bounds a full rotation of the represented geometry with the mounting plate stationary.
The far end of the smooth shaft envelope projects 4 mm from the retaining nut.
