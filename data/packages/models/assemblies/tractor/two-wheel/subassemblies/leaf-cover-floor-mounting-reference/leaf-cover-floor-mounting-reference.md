## Geometry

Nissan Leaf cassette floor and paired covers in the bank frame. Two proposed outboard cover bores overlap the existing stud openings.

Bank-frame millimetres. X is U, Y is N and Z is V. Floor top is V = 0 mm.

Six proposed cover fastening axes were checked against eight existing floor stud openings. The two outboard bores each overlap a stud opening by 0.620 mm. The floor retains its existing geometry.
