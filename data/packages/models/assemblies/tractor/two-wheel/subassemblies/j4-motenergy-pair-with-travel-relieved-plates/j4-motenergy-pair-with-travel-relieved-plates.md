## Geometry

Two Motenergy ME0907 motors with travel-relieved moving plates and relieved fixed backbones.

Millimetres. X along the rails, Y positive left, Z up.

The motor axes are at X = −30 mm and X = 180 mm, both at Z = 430 mm.
The diameter difference between the Ø114.3 mm pilot and Ø115 mm plate opening gives 0.35 mm nominal radial clearance.
