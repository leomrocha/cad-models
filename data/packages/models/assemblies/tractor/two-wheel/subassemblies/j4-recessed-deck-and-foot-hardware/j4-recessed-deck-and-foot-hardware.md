# J4 recessed deck, lifting supports and foot hardware

**Design status** Discussion installation proposal.

## Geometry

Recessed deck, side lifting supports and outboard foot hardware in the J4 chassis frame.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Retained recessed deck mounting positions.

The two deck panels span 600 × 400 mm and share a load plane at Z = 773 mm. The 134 installed solids include 64 deck and support solids and 70 foot hardware solids.
Native measurements confirm two right-front nut bars, four M10 × 35 mm screws and four washers at the recorded outboard foot positions. The chassis supplies both rails and three crossmembers.
