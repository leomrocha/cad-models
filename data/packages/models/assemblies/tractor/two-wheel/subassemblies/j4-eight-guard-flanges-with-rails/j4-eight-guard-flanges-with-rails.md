## Geometry

Both machined J4 longitudinal rails with eight A07 chain-guard flange joints.

Millimetres. X along chassis rails, Y positive left and Z up.

The nominal C129 M6 × 55 screw projects 2.8 mm beyond the locknut. The 34 mm sleeve spans the rail cavity.

The 66 solid occurrences include two rails, eight flange stacks and sixteen wall holes. At each flange the nominal flat rail contact width is 26.7 mm.
