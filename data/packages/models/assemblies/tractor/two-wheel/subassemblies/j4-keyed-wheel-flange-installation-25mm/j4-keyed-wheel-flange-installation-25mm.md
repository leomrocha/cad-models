# J4 keyed Knott wheel flange installation for 25 mm shaft

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-keyed-wheel-flange-installation-25mm`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion assembly proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j4-keyed-wheel-flange-installation-25mm.dwg), SHA-256 `80397a5b60c749465df7c2896d9e9908fbc44b57ae61dd82d098a27301bcd09d`.

## Geometry

J4 25 mm keyed shaft, Knott wheel flange, outer washer and nominal M10 × 25 retainer at the checked installed positions.

Millimetres. Shaft-local +Z runs from the inner shaft face toward the wheel.

The key projects 3 mm above the shaft into a 3.3 mm flange keyway. The end washer has 0.5 mm radial space in the pilot. The screw enters the shaft end by 20 mm, leaving 5 mm nominal tip space. Shaft and flange end faces share the nominal washer plane. Preload distribution depends on the final axial tolerances.
