## Geometry

Paired J4 splash sheets and 17 edge-trim cuts at the proposed outboard positions. The left and right offsets from the earlier sheet poses are 147 and 106 mm.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Paired sheet and trim proposal in outboard service poses.

The left sheet seats at Y = 320.5 mm and the right sheet seats at Y = −279.5 mm. The 80 mm straight outboard removal screen gives a 5.1 mm minimum gap to the sampled host bounds. This spatial check compares two sheets and all 17 trim cuts with 692 stationary bodies per caster layout.
