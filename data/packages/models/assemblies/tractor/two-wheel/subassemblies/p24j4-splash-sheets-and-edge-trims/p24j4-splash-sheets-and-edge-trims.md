**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p24j4-splash-sheets-and-edge-trims.dwg), SHA-256 `a6e8344f7112b6fc8586033b3deb8b86510328039d55834bd87c7f5c18a951a7`.

## Geometry

Two handed J4 splash sheets in their installed poses with seventeen EMKA profile cuts.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Paired sheet and trim proposal.

This proposal contains two sheets and seventeen trim solids in their chassis positions. Eight trim occurrences use lengths revised for the Kelly uprights and the BMW cable opening. The 136 trim-to-trim comparisons allow 0.5 mm extra at each cut end.
