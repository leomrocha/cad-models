# J4 raised Gemini 1S receiver mount on the left rail

Revision 1. Discussion installation proposal. Dimensions are in millimetres. X points forward, Y points left and Z points up.

1. **Included geometry.** The vertical passage rail, 61-solid raised cradle, two risers, a carrier spreader, four nominal fillet envelopes and 18 screw, washer, sleeve and nut occurrences. [Tyro Gemini 1S technical specification](https://www.tyroremotes.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Gemini-EN-1.1.pdf)
2. **Rail stack.** Two M6 × 55 screws run downward at (X, Y) = (−311, 142) and (−289, 142). The sleeves span Z = 503 to 537. Native rim checks confirm both walls and both riser feet. Each modeled tip projects 2.8 mm beyond its nut.
3. **Carrier stack.** Two M6 × 20 screws align with the carrier at (X, Z) = (−335, 638) and (−155, 638). Each modeled tip projects 3.8 mm beyond its nut.
4. **Support fit.** Riser feet seat at Z = 540. Riser ends and the spreader meet at Y = 228. The spreader meets the carrier at Y = 232. The four nominal weld envelopes represent two horizontal 16 mm runs with 3 mm legs per riser. Physical strength and weld sizing require a load review.
5. **Checks.** Eighty-seven native solid occurrences and four checked nominal fastening stacks. DWG SHA-256 `65d2ce041d03e40eb2299279acd79e4ea94c14f071e24bfdfd8fb24b686da0af`.
