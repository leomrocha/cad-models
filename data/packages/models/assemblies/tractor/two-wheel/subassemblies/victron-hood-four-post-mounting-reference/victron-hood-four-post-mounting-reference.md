## Geometry

Two Victron reference terminal hoods on four post mounts. Separate inboard angles leave a 137.2 mm gap between their inner edges.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Bank-frame millimetres. X is U, Y is N and Z is V. Floor top is V = 0 mm.

The inboard post seats lie at X = ±77.1 mm and n = 23 mm. The two angle returns each have two Ø4.5 mm bores at n = 34 and 44 mm. The nearest cup wall has a 1.5 mm projected gap to each inboard angle.
