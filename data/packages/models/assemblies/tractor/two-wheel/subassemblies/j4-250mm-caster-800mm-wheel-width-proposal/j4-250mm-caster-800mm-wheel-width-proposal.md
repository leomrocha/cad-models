# J4 two-wheel tractor base

**Revision 3.** Native base-tractor assembly with permanent front and rear tooling mounts, agricultural drive tyres, protected electrical compartments and a full-length cargo plate.

1. **CAD.** [DWG 2018](j4-250mm-caster-800mm-wheel-width-proposal.dwg). SHA-256 `af785cbb60b59b43e3a3d9c6c0f13ef65a00ea3218e7f28ddb47117b1a25518c`. Native readback recorded 910 solid occurrences.
2. **Running gear.** Two Ø476 × 220 mm agricultural tyres with reproduced tread geometry, retained rim, hub and axle assemblies, and a 250 mm rear support wheel on the direct chassis support. The drive-tyre outer span is 793.56 mm at the nominal unloaded pose.
3. **Access panels.** The 981 × 800 × 3 mm cargo plate covers the full length of the body at Z = 773 mm. The removable side panels sit behind the drive wheels. Four capped accessory receivers fit the rear support beam, with two flush to the deck and two flush to the side panels. Electrical equipment sits inside the panel enclosure.
4. **Tool connection.** Each permanent bulkhead has four Ø28.5 mm pin seats with unchanged axes. The 34 mm fork openings accept the removable cassette eyes. Four Ø28 mm front pins seat their Ø34 mm heads at |Y| = 122 mm. The front M8 × 40 keepers use the proposed Ø13 mm internal-hex cap heads with AF6 drives. The checked straight Ø8 mm driver has 4 mm nominal clearance to the inboard ears and a 120 mm approach region.
5. **Local joint case.** A one-pin screen uses 19.62 kN vertical force, 9.81 kN lateral force and up to 9.81 kN assumed axial force. Calculated weld traction is 186.5 MPa front and 166.5 MPa rear against a provisional 193.6 MPa limit. The actual welding procedure, structural load test and dynamic vehicle balance govern hardware release.
6. **Datums and display.** Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Native roles and layers identify the tyres, rims, hubs, frame, body panels, guards and tooling. The standing display shows the base and permanent tooling interfaces.
