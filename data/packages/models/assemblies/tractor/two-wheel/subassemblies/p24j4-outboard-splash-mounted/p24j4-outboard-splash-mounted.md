# J4 outboard splash panels with supports

**Catalogue draft ID** `p24j4-outboard-splash-mounted`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](p24j4-outboard-splash-mounted.dwg), SHA-256 `a2764acaa7231e3f1dbb5bfbf84963e7524abf26f5110226389c08ca2238ee48`.

## Geometry

Outboard J4 splash assembly with the two removable sheets, 17 edge-trim cuts, 22 outboard support pieces, six existing crossbars and six compression sleeves.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Outboard panel and supports design proposal.

The native placement check found the 20 nominal support interfaces at the planned positions. The lower beams and upper pads provide 12 proposed M6 tapping axes at the two sheet seats.
