## Geometry

J3f raised-frame core with two foot and Kelly carrier passage rails and the retained three crossmembers.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Original J3f chassis frame.

Both rails retain eight Ø11 mm foot axes. Four Ø6.6 mm carrier clamp axes sit at X = −137 and 77 mm and Z = 520 mm. The three crossmembers meet the rail inner faces at their six checked end seats.
