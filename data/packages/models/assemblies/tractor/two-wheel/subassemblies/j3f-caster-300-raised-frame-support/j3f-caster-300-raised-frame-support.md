# J3f same-axis 300 mm caster raised-frame installation

**Catalogue draft ID** `j3f-caster-300-raised-frame-support`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion installation proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j3f-caster-300-raised-frame-support.dwg), SHA-256 `7eaed8f6a7e612d23ccd4fd7e3c95dc214c67d08fb66b4e7bfa73c33cf640170`.

## Geometry

J3F 300 mm caster, mounted at zero tilt with both pins.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Ground-working zero arm setting.

The checked installation contains 46 solid occurrences. It contains the fitted M06 caster and hardware, arm, clevis cheeks, pins, keepers and raised supports. The raised support seats at Z = 500 mm and the nominal tie-to-cheek contact is 680 mm² per side. The compared setting is the zero-tilt position. The source ±20° positions produce head-to-cheek interference.
