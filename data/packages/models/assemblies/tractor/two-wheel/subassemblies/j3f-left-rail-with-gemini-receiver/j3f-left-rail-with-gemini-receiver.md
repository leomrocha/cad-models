1. **Included geometry.** One receiver-ready left rail, one 71-solid Tyro Gemini 1S cradle, two M6 × 55 screws, four M6 washers, two M6 locknuts and two compression sleeves. The cradle retains its cable-access and connector geometry scope. [Tyro Gemini 1S technical specification](https://www.tyroremotes.co.uk/wp-content/uploads/2017/02/Gemini-EN-1.1.pdf)

3. **Stack.** Both sleeves span Y = 133 to 167 and seat between the inner rail walls. The nominal screw tip projects 2.8 mm beyond each modeled nut. The rear sleeve clears the open rail end by 4 mm in the proposed geometry. Fit the sleeves before rail-end closure.
4. **Checks.** The native assembly contains 82 solids. Both E04 axes and both nominal stacks passed. The rail box and the 71 cradle solid boxes showed no positive overlap in the saved pose. DWG SHA-256 `a2214debdf60908383fbe394b9e84611b3785da9089fad400a8ad449149ce039`.
