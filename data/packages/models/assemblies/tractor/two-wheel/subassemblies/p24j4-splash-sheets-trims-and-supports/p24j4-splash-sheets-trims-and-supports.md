## Geometry

Paired J4 splash sheets with 17 edge protectors, six lower brackets, six upper crossbars and six compression sleeves in their proposed installation poses.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Paired sheet, trim and support placement proposal.

The two offset lower brackets per side use the gaps between the deck feet. Each lower pilot sits at Z = 570 mm. The right central upper crossbar axis is at Z = 694 mm. Native readback confirmed 37 placed solids and 306 separate trim-to-support box comparisons.
