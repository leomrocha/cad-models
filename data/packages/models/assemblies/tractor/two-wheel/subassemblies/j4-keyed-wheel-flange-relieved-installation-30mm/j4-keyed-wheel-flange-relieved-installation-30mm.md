# J4 keyed Knott wheel flange with relieved washer for 30 mm shaft

**Catalogue draft ID** `j4-keyed-wheel-flange-relieved-installation-30mm`. Revision 1.

**Design status** Discussion assembly proposal.

[Native DWG 2018](j4-keyed-wheel-flange-relieved-installation-30mm.dwg), SHA-256 `c92ddc682c57fa0858bffcef07efb7768e966a516f2b54873947cb14f3b713c0`.

## Geometry

J4 30 mm keyed shaft, Knott wheel flange, outer washer and nominal M10 × 25 retainer at the checked installed positions.

Millimetres. Shaft-local +Z runs from the inner shaft face toward the wheel.

The key projects 3 mm above the shaft into a 3.3 mm flange keyway. The end washer has 0.5 mm radial space in the pilot. The screw enters the shaft end by 20 mm, leaving 5 mm nominal tip space.
The washer bears on the flange. Its measured relief floor stands 0.5 mm off the nominal shaft end and has 0.2 mm radial reserve to the shaft.
