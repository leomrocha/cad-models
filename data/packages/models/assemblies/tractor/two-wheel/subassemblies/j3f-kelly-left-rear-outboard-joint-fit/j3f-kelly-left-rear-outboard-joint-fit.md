# J3f Kelly left rear outboard joint fit

**Design status** Discussion construction proposal.

## Geometry

Nominal J3f outboard Kelly clamp at the left rear axis, assembled beside the retained Motenergy motor plates.

Millimetres. X forward, Y left and Z up. Left rear Kelly clamp at X = −137 and Z = 520 mm.

The M6 screw passes inward from the outer washer and enters the nominal blind envelope by 10.4 mm. The tip stops 2.6 mm before the blind bottom. The rail insert leaves 22 mm to the fixed motor backbone along Y. Insert retention and actual thread fit remain separate construction inputs.
