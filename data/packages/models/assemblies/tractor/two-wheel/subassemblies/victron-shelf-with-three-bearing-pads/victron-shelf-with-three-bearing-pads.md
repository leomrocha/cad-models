## Geometry

Victron side-mount shelf floor with three 2 mm bearing pads aligned to the main battery case faces. The total nominal bearing area is 2,140 mm².

Shelf-local millimetres. The upper plate face lies at Z = 0 mm and all three pads extend to Z = 2 mm.

Two terminal-side pads and one rear pad bear at Z = 2 mm. Their source planar footprints have a minimum measured edge margin of 1.189 mm. The corresponding rigid case placement lifts the lid 1.956 mm above its zero-pad reference.
