## Geometry

Both J4 raised rails fitted with eight A07 stacks with pocketed feet.

Millimetres. X along chassis rails, Y positive left and Z up.

The nominal C129 M6 × 55 screw projects 2.8 mm beyond the locknut. The 34 mm sleeve spans the rail cavity.

Each rail screw axis is 22 mm forward of the guard mounting axis. The stepped foot and offset support leg share a 30 mm² nominal seat.

The foot pocket ceiling sits at Z = 499 mm, giving 0.75 mm nominal vertical clearance over the original A06 inboard washer.

The 58 solid occurrences include two rails and eight offset stacks. The native checks cover 16 rail wall holes. Each foot has 887.617 mm² of nominal flat rail contact.
