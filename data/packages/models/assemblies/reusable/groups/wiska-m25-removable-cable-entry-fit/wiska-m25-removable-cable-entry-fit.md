# WISKA M25 removable cable entry installation reference

Revision 1. Installation reference for one WISKA ESKV 25 LG in a removable carrier. The dimensions are in millimetres. Z points outward and the insert outer face is Z = 0.

1. **Included geometry.** One 3 mm carrier, one 1.5 mm removable insert, four M4 screws, eight washers, four M4 locknuts, one EADR gasket, one EMUG locknut and one ESKV gland projection. The assembly reuses the saved 18-solid carrier and fixings group.
2. **Nominal placement.** The gland shoulder is Z = +2 mm, its free end is Z = −8 mm, and the gland locknut occupies Z = −7.5 to −1.5 mm. The nominal thread reference projects 0.5 mm beyond that locknut. The M4 screw references project 4.9 mm beyond their M4 locknuts.
3. **Passages.** The Ø26 mm insert opening gives a 0.5 mm radial gap to the nominal Ø25 mm mounting cylinder. The Ø40 mm carrier opening gives a 1.525 mm radial gap to the measured nominal locknut projection.
4. **Gasket fit.** The free-state Ø24 mm gasket bore overlaps the nominal Ø25 mm thread cylinder by 0.5 mm radially. The model records the uncompressed 2 mm axial stack. Gasket deformation, actual thread engagement and sealing performance require part evidence. [WISKA ESKV 25 data sheet](https://www.wiska.com/en/pdf/10066413/ESKV-25.pdf)
5. **Checks.** The saved 21 solids reproduce both plate openings, four M4 fixing stations and the three separate gland-component stations. DWG SHA-256 `5271ff17d412100a95ee534272195e5d0ea896241806bfbe134af15d4d0f9a08`.
