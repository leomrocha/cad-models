## Technical specification

Research date: 11 November 2026. Values below are published manufacturer claims. Loads are per wheel or tyre as labelled by the source; they are not axle ratings.

### Published rim ratings

1. **Moose Racing 387X aluminum ATV wheel:** the Moose 2021 catalog rates its 12×7 in wheel at 675 lb (306 kg) and its 12×8 in wheel at 1,150 lb (522 kg). The catalog gives the same capacities for 14×7 and 14×8 wheels. It does not state a test method, static versus operating condition, speed, inflation pressure, or duty cycle for these rim capacities. The catalog identifies the 387X as aluminum; no separate steel 387X rating was found. Moose 2021 catalog, PDF.

2. **ITP SS212 12-inch ATV wheel:** ITP publishes a 700 lb (318 kg) wheel load rating. The product page does not state the test method, static versus operating condition, speed, or duty cycle. ITP SS212 manufacturer page.

The two published 12×7/12-inch ATV rim examples are 306–318 kg per wheel. This is an observed sample range for those named products and sizes. The Moose 12×8 rating is 522 kg per wheel, showing that the rating depends on the exact rim model and size. The sources do not identify these values as static-only capacities.

### Tyre ratings near the robot wheel diameters

1. **Titan Mud Lite AT, 25×10-12:** Titan publishes 420 lb (191 kg) maximum load at 7 psi and recommends an 8-inch rim. The nominal 25-inch diameter is 635 mm by unit conversion. The cited product page does not give a speed condition alongside this load value. Titan product page.

2. **Apex 25×10-12 ATV tyres:** the Apex catalog lists a 4-ply tyre at 164 kg and 5 psi, and a 6-ply tyre at 189 kg and 7 psi. The catalog gives a 635 mm overall diameter for this size. The cited catalog table does not state speed or duty conditions. Apex ATV tyre catalog, PDF.

3. **Trelleborg T539 TL 20×8.00-10 agricultural tyre:** the project’s existing idea document records a 505 mm outside diameter and 585 kg maximum tyre load at 30 km/h and 2.9 bar for the 6-ply catalogue row. This is a tyre rating, not a rim or hub rating. Trelleborg light-service tyre catalog, PDF; existing project reference: 2-wheel tractor ideas.

### Project wheel candidates and assembly ratings

The current 635 mm-class market candidates include Moose 12×7 steel rims with TnC Pro-MD 25×8-12 and 25×10-12 tyres, plus Moose Racing 387X 12×7 alloy rims with 25-inch tyres. Existing seller records give no load rating for those exact Moose/TnC assemblies or the 387X complete wheel-and-tyre set. The manufacturer rim ratings above apply to the named rim sizes; the Titan and Apex tyre figures apply only to their named tyre models. A combined wheel rating cannot be stated from unlike components without confirming the exact tyre model, rim size, pressure and operating conditions.

Existing HY400 replacement rim and hub listings provide part numbers and prices but no rim, hub or bearing load rating. No standalone published hub/bearing assembly rating was located in the reviewed local records. Vehicle axle or gross-weight ratings are not used to infer a per-wheel capacity.

Local wheel market records reviewed: market wheel candidates, Moose steel rims and TnC Pro-MD tyres, HY400 aluminum rim, and HY400 steel rim. External documents were consulted online; no original files were downloaded.

### Steel ATV rims: follow-up research

Research date: 11 November 2026. The current robot wheel references use nominal 25-inch tyres (635 mm by unit conversion). The rims below are 12-inch products commonly paired with that tyre size. Every load figure in this section is a claimed per-rim capacity; none is a hub, bearing, axle, tyre, or complete-corner rating.

1. **QuadBoss Steely 608489, 12×7 in, 4/110, 5+2 offset:** QuadBoss identifies the rim as high-strength steel and publishes a 1,000 lb (454 kg) load rating. The manufacturer page gives 13 mm lug holes. It does not state center bore, test method, static versus operating condition, speed, pressure, or duty cycle. Manufacturer MSRP is **$68.99 USD**; the manufacturer directs buyers to dealers and does not state tax or delivery charges. QuadBoss manufacturer page. Summit Racing lists the same part at $68.99 USD and reports stock at selected locations; tax and shipping depend on checkout. Summit Racing item listing.

2. **Sedona Steel 570-9002, 12×7 in, 4/110, 5+2 offset:** Dennis Kirk lists a 1,000 lb (454 kg) wheel rating, steel construction, 14 mm tapered stud holes, price **$54.86 USD** (listed MSRP $60.95), and one unit in stock. The listing does not state tax, delivery cost, rating method, speed, or duty cycle. Dennis Kirk item listing.

3. **Sedona Steel 570-9003, 12×7 in, 4/110, 2+5 offset:** Dennis Kirk lists a 1,000 lb (454 kg) wheel rating, steel construction, 14 mm tapered stud holes, price **$54.86 USD** (listed MSRP $60.95), and three units in stock. The listing does not state tax, delivery cost, rating method, speed, or duty cycle. Dennis Kirk item listing.

4. **ITP Delta Steel 12×7 in, 4/110, 2+5 offset, seller MPN 1225544014:** Quad Bikes Wales lists a 300 lb (136 kg) load capacity, steel construction, precision-rolled rim and stamped high-strength center. The item price is **£49.14** (the page also displays £40.95), and the item is out of stock. Its product data do not label the two prices as VAT-inclusive/exclusive and do not state delivery cost, rating method, speed, or duty cycle. ITP's manufacturer page confirms the Delta Steel line and 12×7, 4/110 product configurations but does not publish a load rating. Treat 300 lb as a reseller specification for this exact part, not an ITP manufacturer rating. Quad Bikes Wales item listing; ITP Delta Steel manufacturer page.

The documented sample of steel 12×7 ATV rims spans **136–454 kg per rim** across those exact part numbers. The 136 kg value is a retailer's listing for ITP Delta Steel; QuadBoss publishes 454 kg for its Steely rim, and Sedona retailers publish 454 kg for the 570-9002 and 570-9003 rims. These sources do not state whether their rim ratings are static or operating ratings, and give no common speed or duty condition. The sample is an observed range for the listed products, not a rating for an unlisted rim or wheel assembly.

### Steel wheel construction and European listings

- **Moose Utility Division 120X steel wheel, part 0231-0039:** 12×7 in, 4/110, 5+2. Moose's catalog describes heavy-duty steel construction, reinforced centers, and sealed/welded centers. It gives no load rating. Parts Europe listed the wheel at **€111.05 including 19% VAT**, with **0 in stock** on the observed listing; delivery price was not shown. Moose 2025 catalog, PDF; Parts Europe Moose listing.
- **ITP Delta Steel, 12×7 in, 4/110, 2+5, part 02310021:** Tonnycat listed the steel rim at **€62.18**, displayed beside a €67.60 price, and marked it shipped within 48 hours. The page does not state load rating or tax treatment. Tonnycat ITP item listing.
- **Repair and fabrication statements:** Moose describes the 120X steel rim centers as sealed/welded. ITP describes Delta Steel as having a precision-rolled rim and stamped center. A retailer describes Sedona steel rims as often straightenable or repairable when bent. These are product-source descriptions; the sources do not provide repair procedures or impact-test results.

These listings are rim-only products. The 13 mm or 14 mm lug-hole values describe rim fastening holes; they do not rate or identify a compatible hub. No separate hub or bearing assembly load rating is reported by these rim listings.
